Booking a **private spa for couples** has become one of the most popular wellness experiences in France. According to the French National Wellness Union (Syndicat National du Bien-etre), the spa market grew nearly 8 % between 2023 and 2025, driven by strong demand for intimate and romantic formats. This guide covers included services, regional price ranges, selection criteria and booking best practices.

## What is a private spa for couples?

A private spa for couples is a wellness space sold on an exclusive time slot, accessible only to the two members of the couple during the booked period. Unlike a public spa, where pools and cabins are shared with other clients, the private format guarantees total privacy.

### Private spa, public spa, home spa: the differences

Public spas (aquatic centers, thermal baths, fitness clubs) offer free access to shared pools. They suit clients looking for frequent use and a moderate budget, around 25 to 50 euros per person.

Home spa services send a practitioner to the client's home with portable equipment (massage table, sometimes inflatable jacuzzi). The price is generally aligned with that of an urban private spa.

The private spa for couples combines intimacy, the quality of fixed installations (built-in jacuzzi, infrared sauna, tiled hammam) and hotel-grade services. It is the dominant format for special occasions.

## Typical private spa services

A standard session is built around several pieces of equipment and services:

- Private jacuzzi with hydromassage, usually between 36 and 38 degrees Celsius
- Finnish sauna or infrared cabin
- Tiled hammam, with soft lighting and essential oil diffusion
- Sensory shower with chromatic jets
- Relaxation lounge with loungers, tea and infusions
- Towels, robes and slippers provided
- Welcome drink, sometimes champagne or non-alcoholic cocktail

Premium packages add a duo massage, a body scrub or a facial treatment. The full ritual then lasts between 1.5 and 3 hours. For a deeper dive into duo treatments, see this guide on the [couples massage anniversary gift](/en/blog/best-couples-massage-anniversary-gift/).

## Criteria for choosing the right private spa

Several criteria help arbitrate between venues:

### Duration and format

For a first experience, a 1 to 1.5 hour format is a safe bet. Beyond that, there is a risk of saturation on hot equipment (sauna, hammam). Experienced couples prefer formats with duo treatments to break the pace.

### Privacy and layout

Check that the private cabin has a dedicated entrance, a private changing area and decent sound insulation. Some entry-level venues share corridors or changing rooms between several cabins, which reduces the sense of exclusivity.

### Included services

Compare what is included in the displayed price: drinks, robes, access to a relaxation area after the session, parking. Price gaps between similar venues often come from these extras.

### Location

A downtown private spa makes it easy to combine with a restaurant. Spas located on the outskirts or inside a hotel offer a more immersive weekend format. For a ready-to-give option, check the guide on the [couples spa gift card](/en/blog/best-couples-spa-gift-card/).

## Suitable occasions

The **private spa for couples** suits many occasions:

- Valentine's Day: the most requested slot of the year, booking recommended 6 to 8 weeks in advance
- Couple anniversary: to mark a date without the weight of a full weekend
- Individual birthday gift: gift format valid 6 to 12 months
- Bachelorette or bachelor party as a couple: intimate format before the wedding
- Romantic weekend: combined with a hotel night, often as a package
- After a busy period: mid-duration relaxation ritual

Booking peaks concentrate on three periods: Valentine's Day (February), Mother's Day (May) and end of year (December). Outside these peaks, availability remains wide, even less than a week in advance.

## Booking: best practices

Booking a private spa requires a few precautions:

### Lead time

Plan 2 to 3 weeks ahead for a standard slot, 6 to 8 weeks for Valentine's Day or a Saturday evening in high season. Slots from 6pm to 9pm are the most requested.

### Deposit and cancellation

Most venues require a 30 to 50 % deposit at booking. Cancellation conditions vary: 48 hours for flexible structures, 7 days minimum for 5-star hotels. Always read the conditions before validating payment.

### Gift voucher

Prefer fixed-package vouchers (specific service included) over open-value vouchers, which often require an additional payment on site if prices have changed. Check validity, usually 6 months to 1 year, sometimes extendable.

### Practical checks

Confirm the presence of showers, private changing rooms, and the possibility to bring your own swimwear if needed. Some venues require swimwear for hygiene reasons, others allow nudity in private cabins.

## 2026 trends

The private spa market is evolving fast. Three trends shape the offer in 2026:

- Short formats: 45 to 60 minute sessions are gaining traction, meeting demand for wellness breaks integrated into daily life
- Digital gift vouchers: nearly 65 % of couple bookings come from a gift voucher purchased online, according to Xerfi Wellness France
- Hotel and spa combo: "night plus spa" packages represent a growing share of weekend bookings, with an average price around 350 to 600 euros for two people
