Planning a bachelorette party around a **private spa** comes down to one deceptively simple question: where to book a day that brings the group together in a setting worthy of the occasion. According to the Global Wellness Institute, the global spa economy reached 105 billion dollars in 2022, driven in part by group formats and occasions such as bachelorette parties. Three channels share the market, each with very different logics of price, setting and booking. This comparison reviews them for a group of brides-to-be.

## Comparison of options for booking a bachelorette party spa

Four approaches coexist for booking a private spa day for a group. The table below compares them on the criteria that matter for a bachelorette party: the type of setting, the ability to privatise a space for the group, payment flexibility and geographic coverage.

| Criterion | Sezame | Wonderbox / Smartbox | Staycation | Direct booking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Type of setting | 4-5 star hotel spa | General wellness gift box | Daytime hotel access | Independent private spa |
| Group privatisation | Dedicated spaces and slots | Depends on the provider | Depends on the hotel | Yes, on quote |
| Indicative price / person | 60 to 250 euros | 40 to 130 euros | 50 to 150 euros | 50 to 200 euros |
| Gift card | Yes | Yes, box format | Yes | Depends on the venue |
| Coverage | Paris, PACA, Occitania, Alsace, Normandy, London | Nationwide | Major cities | Local |
| **Verdict** | The high-end hotel setting, multi-city | The entry level and tight budgets | Testing a hotel for the day | The neighbourhood spa already scouted |

The methodology uses four objective criteria: the standing of the venue, the ability to gather the group in a closed space, the price range per person and geographic reach. No single option wins on every count, the right choice depends on the group's profile.

## Private spa versus regular spa: what changes for a bachelorette party

A regular spa hosts several clients at once in the same pools. A private spa reserves the space for the group alone during a set slot. For a bachelorette party, this difference is decisive: it shapes the noise level, the privacy and the ability to personalise the moment.

### Why private wins for a group of brides-to-be

The shared format forces a quiet atmosphere and means sharing the steam room or jacuzzi with strangers. Hard to host a festive moment for eight there. The private format removes that constraint: chosen music, drinks, decoration, free pace. Most private spaces take 2 to 12 people, a range that covers the vast majority of bachelorette groups.

The other advantage is duration. A private spa is booked in blocks of 2 to 3 hours, extendable to a half-day or full day with treatments and a meal. This flexibility makes it easy to match the day to the budget collected from the guests.

## Sezame: luxury hotel spas accessible without an overnight stay

Among the platforms, [Sezame](https://joinsezame.com) holds a particular position: it gives access to luxury hotel spas without any obligation to stay the night. In Paris, that covers venues such as The Peninsula, Le Plaza Athénée, Le Meurice and L'Hôtel du Palais, spas usually reserved for room guests.

For a bachelorette party, the appeal is twofold. The group accesses a 5-star setting for a few hours, at a rate far below an overnight stay. And booking happens online, for a daytime slot, with spaces suited to groups.

### Key features

- Access without an overnight stay: booking a daytime spa slot, without reserving a room, from 60 to 100 euros per person depending on the venue
- Multi-destination coverage: Paris, Antibes, PACA, Occitania, Alsace, Normandy, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, and London recently, useful for a bachelorette party organised outside the capital
- Gift card: the option to lock in a budget before setting the date, handy for a shared fund among friends

The positioning stays high-end, with no discounting logic. A group after the lowest price will lean towards a gift box, but for a hotel setting, the offer is hard to match on the French market.

## Comparative analysis: gift boxes, daycation and direct booking

The three other channels meet distinct needs, each with a clear limit for a bachelorette party.

The **Wonderbox** and **Smartbox** gift boxes start between 40 and 100 euros. Their strength: a low entry price and nationwide distribution, right down to physical retail. Their limit: quality depends entirely on the provider chosen within the box, and luxury hotel spas are rare there. For a bachelorette party demanding on the setting, the box works better as a complement, an individual treatment gifted to the bride-to-be, than as the main booking.

**Staycation** offers access to a hotel and its facilities during the day, a format called daycation. The spa offer exists but stays secondary to the hotel night, and the number of venues per city remains limited. The channel suits testing a hotel, less so privatising a spa for a group.

**Direct booking** with an independent spa, by contacting the venue or through a marketplace like Funbooker, works well when the group already knows the address. The quote is negotiated, the space privatised for a few hours. The weak point: all the research and comparison work falls on the organiser, with no guarantee on the level of service.

> "The private spa segment is currently the most dynamic in the French wellness market, with average growth of 8.2 % per year since 2022, mainly driven by group demand and gift offers." Syndicat National du Bien-être, Wellness Market Observatory 2025.

This momentum explains the multiplication of bachelorette-focused offers, from neighbourhood spas to premium platforms. The same trend shows up in the [private spa couple](/en/blog/private-spa-couple/) format, another category driven by special occasions.

## Which option for your bachelorette party?

The right channel depends on the group's profile, the budget collected and the level of expectation on the setting.

### Small group, tight budget

For 4 to 6 guests with a budget under 80 euros per person, a wellness gift box or an independent spa outside the capital offers the best value. A 2-hour spa access format, without an individual treatment, is enough to create a group moment. A [mother-daughter spa day](/en/blog/mother-daughter-spa-day/) follows the same controlled-budget logic.

### Premium group, standout occasion

To mark the moment with a 5-star setting, a luxury hotel spa platform is the way to go. Expect 150 to 250 euros per person for a half-day with a treatment in a palace hotel. The premium over an urban spa is justified by the venue, the service and the exclusivity of the space.

### Dates not set or a shared fund

When the date is not fixed or payment is split among several people, the gift card solves both problems. Each guest contributes to the amount, and the bride-to-be then picks her slot and venue. It is also the option for a surprise gift, along the lines of a [couples spa gift card](/en/blog/best-couples-spa-gift-card/).

## Booking without mistakes: prices, lead times and pitfalls

The budget for a bachelorette party private spa day varies by duration and level of service.

| Package | Duration | Indicative price / person |
|---|---|---|
| Private spa access only | 2 to 3 hours | 60 to 100 euros |
| Private spa and treatment (massage, scrub) | Half-day | 120 to 180 euros |
| Full day in a luxury hotel (spa, treatments, lunch) | Full day | From 250 euros |

On lead time, 3 to 4 weeks is enough for a weekday slot, against 6 to 8 weeks for a Saturday or the high season from May to September. The best addresses are easy to compare beforehand through a [website to book a spa](/en/blog/where-to-book-a-spa/) that centralises availability.

### Pitfalls to avoid

1. Booking a shared spa thinking it is private: explicitly check the "private" or "privatisation" mention before paying
2. Overlooking the deposit and cancellation terms: most venues ask for 30 to 50 % at booking, with cancellation windows of 48 hours to 7 days
3. Choosing a gift box in euros rather than a set package: the euro voucher often forces a top-up on site if prices have changed, while the package fixes the content in advance
