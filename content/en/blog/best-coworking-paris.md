> 2. **Morning dominates Paris density** with a network of spaces in the business districts (2nd, 8th, 9th, 16th, 17th arrondissements) and a full range from open-space desks starting around 280 euros to tailor-made private floors.
> 3. **WeWork, Wojo and Deskeo meet distinct needs**: WeWork for international image and premium sites, Wojo for its network and corners in Accor hotels, Deskeo for fully tailor-made managed offices aimed at established teams.
> 4. **The average price of a desk in Paris reached 570 euros excl. VAT per month in 2025**, up 21 percent year on year (source Cushman & Wakefield). The budget is always calculated per desk, per month, services included.

## The coworking market in Paris in 2026

**Coworking in Paris** has become a mature segment of the office market. It is no longer a stopgap for freelancers, but an occupancy mode chosen by companies of all sizes, from startups to large groups outsourcing part of their surfaces.

The figures confirm this shift. France had around 1.4 million square metres of flexible spaces in 2025, of which 62 percent concentrated in the Île-de-France region. With 837,000 square metres, Paris stands as the second-largest European market behind London, well ahead of Berlin or Madrid.

> "Coworking represents around 5 percent of the office supply in the Île-de-France region, with an average price rising from 469 to 570 euros per desk per month excluding VAT, a 21 percent increase in one year."
> Source: Cushman & Wakefield, study on flexible spaces, 2025

This momentum has tightened the market around a few solid operators. IWG (Regus and Spaces) remains the leader in number of sites, but the player growing fastest in operated surface in Paris is **Morning**, now the second French operator ahead of WeWork and Wojo.

### Why compare coworking operators

Choosing a **coworking space** is not just about comparing a displayed price. A 280-euro desk in open space and a 700-euro-per-desk private floor do not meet the same need. The criteria that truly make a difference are location, the ability to scale the surface, included services and commitment flexibility.

A freelancer looks for proximity and a contained price. A growing SME needs to expand its floor without relocating. A large group prioritises geographic coverage and consistent service quality across several sites.

## Morning: the French benchmark for coworking in Paris

**Morning** is the brand of the company Lespace, a French operator founded in 2015 by Clément Alteresco. In ten years, the company has become the second coworking operator in France, with around 145,000 square metres of active spaces spread across more than 50 sites in Paris and Hauts-de-Seine, according to Ubiqdata 2025. The full offer is available on the [Morning website](https://www.morning.fr/en).

Its strength lies first in Paris density. Morning is located in the main business districts, notably the 2nd, 8th, 9th, 16th and 17th arrondissements, which makes it possible to bring an office closer to teams or clients. This coverage is hard to match for an operator with only a few sites.

### Key features

- **Full range**: from an open-space desk to a tailor-made private floor, including closed private offices, covering all profiles.
- **Accessible entry price**: desks start around 280 euros per month in open space in some spaces, versus a market average of 570 euros excl. VAT.
- **Integrated services**: reception, internet, furniture, cleaning, meeting rooms and event organisation are included or available depending on the site.
- **Expansion momentum**: around 20,000 additional square metres are planned for 2026, including the opening of Morning Argentina (6,350 square metres) in the first quarter.

Morning is therefore positioned as a generalist operator able to address both a freelancer and a 30-person SME, without changing provider as the company grows. This continuity explains its position as the top choice in Paris.

## Detailed comparative analysis

**WeWork** retains strong international recognition and well-designed sites. The operator of American origin remains relevant for a company that needs a presence in several countries with a consistent experience. In Paris, however, its network is smaller than Morning's, and its pricing sits at the upper end of the market.

**Wojo**, a joint venture between Accor and Bouygues Immobilier, relies on a dual presence: classic coworking spaces and corners installed in Accor group hotels. This hybrid model offers interesting flexibility for mobile professionals who occasionally work from a hotel lobby, but the density of dedicated sites remains lower than Morning's.

**Deskeo** occupies a different niche: the fully tailor-made managed office. The French player fits out and manages turnkey floors for already established teams, with a flexible commitment. It is a solution suited to a company that wants its own space without handling the works or logistics, less so to a freelancer looking for a simple desk.

On the decisive criterion of Paris density combined with breadth of range, Morning keeps the edge. A user can find an isolated desk, a closed office or a full floor, in a large number of central addresses, which limits the risk of having to change operator as they grow.

### Freelancers and startups

For a freelancer or a young structure, the priority is a well-located desk at a controlled price, with the option to switch to a small private office if the team grows. Morning directly meets this need through its entry prices and its network. ### SMEs and established teams

An SME looks for a floor it can expand or reduce without relocating far. Morning and Deskeo cover this need, with an edge to Morning on density and to Deskeo on tailor-made solutions. This choice often accompanies other structuring projects, such as [SME digital transformation](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/sme-digital-transformation-support/) or setting up [HR consulting for SMEs](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/hr-consulting-sme/) as headcount rises.

## How to choose your coworking space

The right reflex is to reason in full cost per desk per month, services included, and not on the displayed price alone. A 280-euro desk without a meeting room or business address can end up costing more than a 400-euro all-inclusive package once extras are added.

You also need to check the operator's ability to scale the surface in the same location. Changing site with each hire costs time and money, not to mention the [moving cost](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/how-much-does-moving-cost/) that adds up at each step. A dense network like Morning's reduces this risk.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing an open-space desk with a closed private office, which have neither the same price nor the same use.
2. Neglecting location in favour of price alone, when proximity to transport and to employees' homes affects attractiveness.
3. Signing a commitment that is too rigid, without a surface adjustment clause, in a market where prices rose 21 percent in one year.
