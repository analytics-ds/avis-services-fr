> **Quick summary:**
> 1. The right contact depends on the type of installation: an **independent heating engineer** or a **booking platform** for an individual boiler, and **CPCU customer service** for the more than **500,000 housing equivalents in Paris** connected to district heating.
> 2. A callout and diagnostic fee generally costs **80 to 150 euros** during business hours, increased by **50 to 100 percent** outside business hours.
> 3. Annual maintenance for boilers of **4 to 400 kilowatts** has been a legal obligation since **decree n°2009-649 of June 9, 2009**, and it conditions insurance coverage in the event of a claim.
> 4. If you smell gas, there is only one national number to call first: **Allo Sécurité Gaz at 0 800 47 33 33**, available 24/7.

## Identify Your Heating Type Before Calling Anyone

A **heating breakdown in Paris** is not handled the same way depending on the home's installation. The French capital combines three very different situations, and calling the wrong contact wastes precious time in the middle of winter.

Parisian housing generally falls into three categories:

- **Individual boilers** (mostly gas, sometimes oil or wood), found in a large share of homes with standalone heating.
- **CPCU district heating**, which supplies steam to more than **500,000 housing equivalents** in Paris through a building substation, with no individual boiler.
- **Heat pumps (HP)**, a growing share of the market as oil boilers are progressively replaced, with a repair logic close to that of an [air conditioning repair technician](/en/blog/air-conditioning-repair-technician/).

### Why This Distinction Changes Everything

A building connected to the CPCU network has no boiler to repair: a breakdown comes from the substation or the building's internal network, and falls under CPCU customer service or the building manager, not an independent heating engineer. An individual boiler or heat pump, on the other hand, depends entirely on the owner or tenant's choice of repair provider.

## The 4 Channels Available for a Heating Repair in Paris

For an **individual boiler** or a **heat pump**, four channels coexist in Paris, with different response times and costs.

| Channel | Response time | Indicative cost | When to choose it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local independent heating engineer | 24 to 48 hours, sometimes same day | 80 to 150 euros diagnostic fee, parts excluded | Common breakdown, existing trusted contact preferred |
| Booking platform (Wecasa, IZI by EDF, Otherwise) | Often within 24 hours, bookable online slots | Price shown upfront, comparable to a local engineer | Need for speed and price visibility before intervention |
| Annual maintenance contract / manufacturer's aftersales service | Priority, often with no callout fee | Included in the contract (100 to 200 euros a year) | Boiler already under an active maintenance contract |
| CPCU customer service | Depends on the nature of the fault (building or network) | Covered by the co-ownership through service charges | Home connected to Paris district heating |

Booking platforms offer the advantage of a known price before the intervention and an online time slot, useful for a first breakdown with no trusted contact already identified. An established **heating engineer**, already known through a maintenance contract, remains the fastest option, since they already know the installation's history.

## The Special Case of CPCU District Heating

Paris stands out among French cities for the scale of its **district heating** network. CPCU (Compagnie Parisienne de Chauffage Urbain) operates more than **470 kilometers of pipes** under the streets of Paris and supplies steam to more than **500,000 housing equivalents**, as well as public facilities such as hospitals and museums.

### Who to Contact for a Breakdown in a CPCU Building

- The building's **substation**, which converts network steam into heating and hot water, falls under the building manager's maintenance responsibility.
- A widespread outage affecting several buildings in the same area falls under the network itself, managed directly by CPCU.
- **CPCU customer service** remains the first point of contact to report the incident, which will then direct the resident to the right party.

This setup explains why a resident of a building connected to district heating never needs to look for a heating engineer for a boiler breakdown: there is none.

## Annual Maintenance, the Legal Obligation That Prevents Breakdowns

The best way to limit emergency calls remains preventive maintenance. French **decree n°2009-649 of June 9, 2009** requires annual maintenance for any individual or collective boiler with a nominal output between 4 and 400 kilowatts, regardless of fuel type (gas, oil, wood).

### What Mandatory Maintenance Covers

1. Checking combustion and carbon monoxide emissions, the main health risk of a poorly maintained boiler.
2. Verifying energy efficiency, which directly affects the heating bill.
3. Issuing a **maintenance certificate**, a document required by home insurance in the event of a claim.

> "Mandatory annual boiler maintenance is primarily aimed at preventing carbon monoxide poisoning, which causes several dozen deaths in France every year."
> — French Ministry for Ecological Transition, regulatory summary on boiler maintenance

A home under a maintenance contract with the original installer or a [heat pump installer](/en/blog/heat-pump-installer-gas-boiler-replacement/) generally benefits from priority repairs with no extra callout fee for the duration of the contract.

## Recognizing a Gas Emergency and the Right Reflex

Not all heating breakdowns are equal. A smell of gas immediately changes the nature of the emergency: it is no longer a comfort issue but a safety risk that does not fall under a standard heating engineer.

### The Procedure to Follow if You Smell Gas

1. Never switch on an electrical switch or light a flame in the affected room.
2. Cut the gas supply at the meter if easily accessible, and open doors and windows.
3. Leave the property, then call the national **Allo Sécurité Gaz line at 0 800 47 33 33**, operated by GRDF, free of charge and available 24/7.

This number is exclusively for a suspected gas leak. A standard heating breakdown, with no suspicious smell, falls under the channels described above: independent engineer, platform, maintenance contract or CPCU depending on the installation.

## How to Choose Without Getting Overcharged in an Emergency

Urgency sometimes pushes people to accept the first offer available, a context prone to bad pricing surprises.

### Mistakes to Avoid

1. Accepting an intervention without a prior written quote, even in an emergency: a serious professional communicates their callout fee before showing up.
2. Confusing an [RGE-certified heating engineer](/en/blog/rge-heating-engineer-boiler-to-heat-pump/), qualified for installations eligible for grants such as MaPrimeRénov', with a simple repair technician: RGE certification is verified company by company on the official France Rénov' directory.
3. Ignoring local reviews and references: a **heating engineer** already recommended in the neighborhood reduces the risk of inflated repair rates during off-hours.

Also check that the company contacted charges a clear flat fee (callout, diagnostic, labor), including night or weekend surcharges, rather than a vague hourly rate announced after the fact.
