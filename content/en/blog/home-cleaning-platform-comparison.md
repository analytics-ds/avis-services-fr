## Understanding the home cleaning market in France

**Home cleaning** has historically been the top spending category in personal services in France, ahead of childcare and elderly care. The arrival of digital platforms starting in 2015 has profoundly transformed this market, enabling instant online booking where finding a housekeeper previously required word of mouth or registering at a physical agency.

Three business models coexist today, each answering a different need: one-off booking in a platform-economy style, the traditional agency-managed model with human support, and a subscription with a loyal, dedicated worker.

### The three home cleaning platform models

- **Instant booking**: book online in a few clicks, a different worker possible at each visit
- **Agency-managed mode**: personalized follow-up, contract framed by a physical structure
- **Subscription with a dedicated worker**: same worker at every service, consistency and trust

## Wecasa: platform-style instant booking

Wecasa imported the instant booking model from ride-hailing platforms to the home cleaning sector: the client selects a time slot online, pays by card, and an independent professional performs the service without any employment contract linking the client to the provider. This model appeals to an urban clientele looking for flexibility and no commitment.

### Advantages and limitations of the Wecasa model

- **Advantage**: booking in a few minutes, with no administrative formality, cancellation generally possible up to 24-48h in advance
- **Advantage**: no employment contract or payslip management for the client
- **Limitation**: no guarantee of getting the same professional at every visit, unless explicitly requested
- **Limitation**: the provider's self-employed status implies different social coverage compared to a regular employee

## Shiva: the historic agency-managed model

Shiva has established itself since the 2000s as the reference for the agency-managed model for home cleaning, with a network of 450 physical branches in France. In this model, the individual becomes the legal employer of the worker, but Shiva's branch handles profile selection, drafting the employment contract, and administrative management (declarations, replacements in case of absence).

## Otherwise: the subscription with a dedicated worker

Otherwise built its positioning around a simple promise: offer a monthly subscription with the same cleaning person durably assigned to the household, rather than a different professional at each booking. This model targets families who prioritize a trusted relationship and the worker's familiarity with the home (habits, product locations, pets) over pure flexibility.

### Who the subscription model suits

1. **Households with a regular need** — weekly or biweekly cleaning rather than occasional
2. **Families with children or pets** — a fixed worker's familiarity with the home simplifies the service
3. **Looking for stability** — less suited to occasional needs or secondary residences

## The tax credit: a lever common to all three models

Regardless of the chosen platform, home cleaning remains eligible for the 50 percent tax credit applicable to personal services, capped at 12,000 euros of annual spending (a maximum tax credit of 6,000 euros), increased by 1,500 euros per dependent child or person over 65 in the household, within an overall cap of 15,000 euros of spending.

This scheme applies provided the provider (self-employed contractor, agency-managed branch, or service company) is registered or approved as a personal services provider.
