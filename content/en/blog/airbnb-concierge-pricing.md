## Understanding the Airbnb concierge economic model

An **Airbnb concierge** manages all operations linked to the short-term rental of a property on behalf of an owner. The profession structured itself in France from 2015 with the arrival of GuestReady and BnbLord, in the wake of the explosion of Airbnb and Booking. The French market represents approximately 850 million euros in revenue in 2025 according to the Xerfi study on touristic furnished rentals.

Two pricing models coexist:

- **Revenue percentage model** (most common): the concierge charges between 15 and 25 percent of gross rental income. Advantage: aligns the concierge's interest with property performance. Disadvantage: can become expensive for high-rotation properties.
- **Fixed monthly flat-rate model**: the concierge charges a fixed amount (250 to 600 euros per month depending on city and service level) regardless of revenue. Advantage: cost predictability. Disadvantage: no incentive to optimize pricing.

Some concierges offer a hybrid model: a reduced base flat rate (150 to 250 euros) plus a low percentage (5 to 10 percent) on revenue.

### What the price actually includes

Beyond the percentage or flat rate, precisely verify what is included and what is charged additionally:

- Listing and professional photos (sometimes included, sometimes 200 to 500 euros at creation).
- Calendar and dynamic pricing management (generally included).
- 24/7 traveler communication (included).
- Physical check-in (included or 30 to 50 euros per check-in).
- Cleaning between each rental (often 25 to 70 euros refacturé, sometimes included).
- Linen and consumables (generally refacturé at cost or as an option).
- First-level maintenance (included for small incidents).
- Claims management and insurance declaration (often as a paid option).
- Tax declaration and accounting (paid option in most cases).

## Why use an Airbnb concierge service

Three situations mainly justify the use of a professional Airbnb concierge.

### Situation 1: owner far from the property

For an owner who does not live in the same city as their property (second home, remote rental investment, expatriation), the concierge is practically essential. Without it, it is impossible to manage check-ins, unexpected events and cleaning. The cost of the concierge then becomes an essential service rather than an option.

### Situation 2: high rental volume

Above 10 nights rented per month, management time becomes significant (about 8 to 12 hours of work per month for a heavily rented property). Outsourcing to a concierge frees up this time and professionalizes the offer, which generally increases occupancy rate and average price.

### Situation 3: revenue optimization

Properties managed by a concierge using dynamic pricing (daily price adjustment according to demand, local events, season) generate on average 18 to 25 percent additional revenue according to the AirDNA 2024 study, without increasing occupancy rate. For a Paris property with 30,000 euros annual revenue, this represents 5,400 to 7,500 gross euros in additional income.

## Detailed comparison of the 4 main players

GuestReady has been operating since 2016 in 8 major French cities (Paris, Lyon, Bordeaux, Marseille, Nice, Cannes, Toulouse, Lille). The commission of 18 to 22 percent is in the upper market average, but the service is deemed reliable (Trustpilot score of 4.4 out of 5 over a rolling 12 months in May 2026). Specialty: algorithmic dynamic pricing and professional photography included.

BnbLord is the historical French player (founded 2014, acquired by Sweet Inn in 2021 then resold in 2024). Coverage limited to Paris, Lyon and Bordeaux but very complete service including cleaning in commission. Commission of 20 to 25 percent, justified by inclusion of cleaning and optional linen. Trustpilot score 4.2 out of 5.

HostnFly stands out for its fixed flat-rate model, ideal for owners who want predictability and low cost. Flat rate between 250 and 600 euros per month depending on city and property size. Widest coverage (50 cities). Service deemed more operational than premium. Trustpilot score 4.1 out of 5.

Sweet Inn targets the premium and luxury segment: high-end apartments in Paris, Cannes, Nice, sometimes Saint-Tropez. High commission (22 to 28 percent) but all-inclusive service with personalized welcome, welcome baskets, concierge for travelers. The price-quality ratio is justified only on properties above 200 euros per night.

## Practical tips before signing with a concierge

Five vigilance points help secure the choice of an Airbnb concierge. To explore personal services and their selection criteria more broadly, the article [best personal care company](/en/blog/best-personal-care-company/) lists the main players.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Signing a management mandate without reading the termination clause: some concierges impose 12 months of commitment with 3-month notice.
2. Not verifying who receives traveler payments: some concierges directly collect then refund (risk of delays), others route through the owner's account.
3. Underestimating hidden fees (refacturé cleaning, linen, photos, tax declaration) which can add 5 to 10 points to the displayed commission.
4. Choosing solely on the lowest percentage: a 15 percent commission without dynamic pricing often generates less than a 22 percent commission with tariff optimization.
5. Not verifying regulatory compliance: change of use at the town hall for Paris (above 120 nights per year in primary residence), mandatory registration at the town hall in some cities.
