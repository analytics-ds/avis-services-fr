## Why regularly clean your fabric sofa

A fabric sofa accumulates an average of 2 to 4 grams of dust and allergens per week according to a 2023 ADEME study on indoor air quality. Add to this everyday stains (food, drinks, pets), body oils, and dust mites that thrive in the fibers. Regular **fabric sofa cleaning** addresses three issues: hygiene (allergies, asthma), aesthetics (preserving color and texture) and furniture longevity.

The French furniture industry estimates that a properly maintained sofa lasts 10 to 15 years versus 6 to 8 years for a neglected one, a 30 to 40 percent durability gain. On a mid-range sofa worth 1,200 euros, this represents indirect savings of 400 to 500 euros over 10 years.

### Read the care label first

Every fabric sofa carries a label with a code that determines authorized methods:

- **W**: water cleaning allowed (Marseille soap, gentle steam, injection-extraction).
- **S**: solvent only (mandatory professional dry cleaning).
- **WS**: water and solvent allowed (the most versatile).
- **X**: vacuum only, no liquid products.

Ignoring this indication can cause permanent discoloration or fabric shrinkage.

## DIY method: home fabric sofa cleaning

The DIY method covers most everyday maintenance needs. It works on W and WS codes, with basic household products.

### Steps for a complete DIY fabric sofa cleaning

1. Meticulously vacuum the sofa, including corners and under cushions, with a brush attachment.
2. Generously sprinkle baking soda over the entire surface, let sit 30 to 60 minutes.
3. Vacuum the baking soda.
4. Prepare a Marseille soap solution (1 tablespoon grated in 1 liter of warm water).
5. Lightly soak a white microfiber cloth, wring out firmly, apply on the fabric in circular motions.
6. Rinse with a second cloth dampened with clear water.
7. Dry with a fan or by airing the room at least 4 hours.

For localized stains, treat immediately with salt (red wine), Fuller's earth (grease), or 50 percent diluted white vinegar (ink, marker). ## Renting an injection-extraction machine

For deep cleaning without calling a professional, renting an injection-extraction machine is a good compromise. The principle: inject hot water with detergent into the fibers, then immediately vacuum with the dissolved soiling.

The main rental companies in France are Rug Doctor (present in 1,200 supermarkets including Carrefour, Leclerc, Intermarché), Kärcher (rental at Kiloutou and Loxam) and Vileda (in some hardware superstores). The average rate is 35 to 50 euros per day, plus 10 to 15 euros of dedicated detergent.

Practical tips:

- Test the machine on a hidden area for 10 minutes to check for discoloration.
- Do not over-saturate the fabric: a quick pass beats a long pass.
- Perform 2 to 3 vacuum passes to extract maximum residual water.
- Plan 4 to 6 hours of drying before reusing the sofa.

## Practical tips for maintaining your fabric sofa

Beyond deep cleaning, a few simple gestures preserve a fabric sofa daily.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Rubbing a fresh stain: this pushes it deeper into the fibers. Always blot.
2. Using bleach: guaranteed irreversible discoloration.
3. Saturating the fabric with water: risk of mold in the internal foam.
4. Ignoring the label: testing a forbidden product can ruin the sofa.
5. Waiting too long before treating a stain: after 48 hours, the stain sets and becomes much harder to remove.
