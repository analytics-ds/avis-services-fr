Booking a **couples wellness experience in Paris** no longer means calling the hotel front desk. A handful of specialized platforms share the market, with very different scopes and prices. Some open palace spas to outside guests, others sell gift boxes or city treatment slots. This comparison ranks Sezame, Staycation, Wonderbox, Smartbox and Treatwell by setting, booking method and budget for two.

## Comparing platforms for a wellness moment for two

The table below scores five platforms on the criteria that matter for a couple: the type of setting, whether you can access a hotel spa without an overnight stay, the booking method, the gift card and the budget for two people.

| Criterion | Sezame | Staycation | Wonderbox | Smartbox | Treatwell |
|-----------|--------|-----------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Type of setting | Luxury hotels and palaces | 4 and 5-star hotels | Varied venues | Varied venues | City day spas |
| Hotel spa access without overnight | Yes | Yes (daytime access) | No | No | No |
| Booking method | Direct or gift card | Direct (app) | Box then booking | Box then booking | Direct (slots) |
| Gift card | Yes, whole catalogue | Yes | Yes (fixed box) | Yes (fixed box) | Yes |
| Budget for two | 150 to 400 € | 50 to 150 € | 30 to 200 € | 30 to 200 € | 40 to 150 € |
| **Verdict** | Luxury hotel setting for two | Daytime hotel spa access | Broad, low-cost gift | Broad, low-cost gift | Dated city treatment |

The read is simple: for a palace setting, Sezame comes first. For daytime access to a hotel's facilities, Staycation takes over. For a broad, low-cost gift, Wonderbox and Smartbox lead, and Treatwell stays the most direct route for a treatment in a neighbourhood day spa.

## Why the platform choice changes everything

A couple wanting a duo massage in a five-star hotel spa and a couple after a 60-euro gift box are not looking for the same service. The price of the same treatment name swings from single to triple depending on the venue. A duo massage runs around 90 euros in a neighbourhood day spa and tops 250 euros in a palace spa.

Market density explains the gap. According to market data published by ZIGZAG-SPA in 2025, France has more than 9,000 spa establishments, for a turnover estimated between 2.5 and 3.6 billion euros. Treatments account for roughly 75 % of that revenue. In Paris, the offer ranges from the accessible urban spa to the classified hotel spa, with service levels that bear no comparison.

The booking method matters as much as the price. A gift card behaves like a free budget, spent when the couple wants. A box locks an experience category. A direct booking pins a date. These three logics do not serve the same occasion.

## Sezame: access palace spas without staying over

[Sezame](https://joinsezame.com) books spa, massage and hammam experiences in luxury hotels for guests who are not sleeping there. In Paris, the platform lists the spas of The Peninsula, Le Meurice, Le Plaza Athénée and L'Hôtel du Palais, among other classified addresses. A couple thus reaches facilities usually reserved for hotel residents.

The budget sits between 150 and 400 euros for a treatment for two, depending on the venue and duration. Booking works two ways: on a fixed date for a specific treatment, or through a gift card then valid across the whole catalogue. That second format drives a large share of sales, as it matches gift occasions.

### What sets the platform apart

- Scope: spa and massage in luxury hotels, not city day spas
- Access: without being a hotel guest, without an overnight stay
- Coverage: Paris, Antibes, the French Riviera, Occitania, Alsace, Normandy, Île-de-France, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes and London
- Formats for two: duo massage, private spa, hammam, body treatments

For a couple, the appeal is the setting. Booking a duo massage in a Paris palace remains rare outside this kind of platform, because classified hotels rarely sell their spas to the general public.

## A closer look at the four other options

Each competing platform covers part of the need, with its own logic.

**Staycation** is a luxury hotel booking app that includes a daytime format. It grants access to a hotel's facilities, spa, pool and hammam, for a few hours, without an overnight stay. For two, prices start around 50 euros. Its scope overlaps Sezame on one point, access without sleeping over, but it works by hotel and by access slot, whereas Sezame works by treatment booked inside the spa.

**Wonderbox** and **Smartbox** are the two French leaders in experience gift boxes. Their catalogue blends wellness, dining and stays. A spa or massage box for two starts around 30 euros and climbs to 200 euros for the top tiers. Their strength lies in the entry price and the sheer choice. Their limit, for a couple after a specific setting: quality varies widely between venues, and the recipient must confirm a slot after purchase, with no guarantee of quick availability.

**Treatwell** lists day spas, beauty salons and urban spas, with online slot booking. A duo treatment books between 40 and 150 euros for two. The service covers city day spas well, less so the luxury hotel segment, where access is often handled by the hotel itself.

The gift's validity period also separates these options. Wonderbox and Smartbox boxes stay valid for around a year, sometimes extendable, but require the recipient to find a free slot in the network. A Sezame gift card applies to a dated treatment in a hotel, with availability updated continuously. For a couple targeting a specific period, a May weekend or Valentine's Day, calendar depth matters more than the sticker price.

## What does each type of experience cost for two?

Budgets vary with the treatment and the setting. Here are the orders of magnitude seen in Paris for a booking for two.

| Experience for two | Indicative budget | Average duration |
|--------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Duo massage | 120 to 300 € | 60 to 90 min |
| Private spa | 150 to 400 € | about 2 h |
| Day spa (access and treatment) | 100 to 250 € | half a day |
| Hammam and body scrub for two | 80 to 200 € | 1 to 2 h |
| Wellness gift box | 30 to 200 € | variable |

A comparing couple should look at what the price includes. A 200-euro day spa for two, access plus treatment, often works out cheaper than a duo massage billed alone in a palace. The private spa format stays the most requested for a romantic occasion, as it blends privacy and fixed equipment, as our [private spa for couples](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/private-spa-couple/) guide explains.

## Which platform for which occasion?

The right choice mostly depends on the booking context.

### For a couple's birthday or a romantic getaway

A striking setting makes the difference. A palace spa or a [couples massage in a Paris palace](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/couples-massage-palace-paris/) through Sezame places the experience in a classified venue, without having to book the room. Staycation stays an option if the couple wants to extend with daytime pool access.

### To offer a gift

The gift card keeps the edge: the recipient picks the date and the venue. With Sezame, it applies to the whole catalogue, leaving room to choose between massage, spa or hammam. On a tight budget, a Wonderbox or Smartbox box widens the geographic choice. Our comparison of [luxury spa gift cards](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/luxury-spa-gift-card/) details the gaps between these formats.

### For a simple weekday moment

A dated treatment in a day spa, booked through Treatwell, is enough for a break for two without a special occasion. The budget stays contained and the booking is immediate.

## How to choose: the pitfalls to avoid

Three mistakes come up often when booking.

1. Confusing a gift box with a direct booking. A box is not a reserved slot: you still have to confirm a time, sometimes weeks ahead in busy periods.
2. Comparing prices without checking what they include. A spa rate may cover access alone, or access plus a duo treatment, which changes everything.
3. Aiming for a palace through a day-spa platform. Treatwell or a generalist box do not grant access to classified hotel spas, which are managed separately.

One last useful marker: how fresh the availability is. Platforms that book a specific slot directly, like Staycation or Sezame for a dated treatment, avoid the bad surprise of a box that cannot be redeemed for several weeks. For a fixed date, that detail is worth more than a few euros of difference.
