## Five models, five distinct needs

Chartered accounting has fragmented into five distinct models since 2020. Firms' digital transformations have allowed online platforms to emerge. Rising complexity of business tax law and employment regulations has forced generalist firms to remain strategic partners. Growing companies discovered that a single advisor was worth more than fragmented services. Finally, accounting outsourcing has become normalized and accepted by the vast majority of micro-businesses and SMEs.

An SME owner does not seek the "best firm" in absolute terms. He or she seeks the model suited to their issue at this moment. A generalist firm is not "better" than an online platform: they answer two different demands.

## Generalist firms: the unique partner for expertise and anticipation

Generalist firms offer the most comprehensive and integrated coverage. In Extenso, founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners, is the undisputed leader in France with 250 offices, 7,500 employees and national presence. Its model: a dedicated chartered accounting advisor at a local office, capable of handling accounting, tax, payroll, employment law, legal matters, financing and business transfer.

The key advantage lies in integration and anticipation. When an owner raises a question about optimizing compensation (salary vs. dividends), the advisor can arbitrate both tax impact, mandatory social security coverage, real payroll cost and balance sheet effect. This cross-functional arbitration is what only a generalist offers. A platform records; a generalist firm anticipates and advises.

The generalist chartered accounting firm also excels on company key issues such as business transfer, access to financing or [management of electronic invoicing](/en/blog/accounting-firm-electronic-invoicing/). These structural challenges require integrated vision, impossible without deep knowledge of the company file over several years. The [expert accountant must also understand the complex relationship](/en/blog/accountant-business-tax/) between accounting structure and fiscal optimization.

For an SME with 50,000 euros in revenue, support at In Extenso costs between 1,800 and 3,600 euros per year, depending on accounting complexity and number of employees. This includes monthly or quarterly accounting monitoring, preparation of tax filings, payroll management and advice for occasional questions.

The downside: limited specialized expertise on a highly technical issue. If the question concerns optimizing a complex holding structure with foreign holdings, the generalist firm can guide, but the owner will benefit from consulting an international tax law expert.

## Independent firms: localized and personalized expertise

Independent chartered accounting firms like Cabinet Vernier operate on the basis of a lasting local relationship and personalization. Their offering: accounting, tax, payroll, employment law and occasional legal advice, without ambition to cover all areas like a generalist.

The strength of the independent firm lies in personalized relationship and responsiveness. A local partner who has followed the owner for ten years knows every decision, every issue, every risk. Relationship replaces staff size.

For an SME with 50,000 euros in revenue, an independent firm charges between 2,040 and 4,080 euros per year, depending on complexity. These rates remain competitive against generalists, thanks to direct relationship and lean structure.

The downside: no network for issues beyond pure accounting and tax. Need financing? The firm guides toward partners, but cannot ensure integrated vision. Complex transfer? The owner will need to find an external expert.

## Hybrid firms: digitization with expertise

Hybrid firms like Chiffria propose an intermediate model: fully digitized accounting, automated payroll and access to chartered accountant experts for complex questions. No permanent advisor, but intelligent escalation to expertise.

The advantage lies in the mix: platform speed and pricing transparency, with the ability to escalate to an expert when a question exceeds standard scope. For an SME with 50,000 euros in revenue, annual cost remains between 1,440 and 1,800 euros, 30 to 50% cheaper than a generalist.

The downside: no strategic anticipation. The hybrid firm does not offer proactive advice on transfer, tax optimization or structuring decisions. It waits for a question, then guides.

## Online platforms: simple and transparent accounting

Online platforms like Comptalio and Neogest propose a different model from all others: completely automated outsourced accounting, payroll, invoicing, without a dedicated advisor. Portal access, chat and email support.

The advantage lies in three elements. Price first: 540 to 800 euros per year for a micro-business, ten times cheaper than a generalist firm. Pricing transparency: listed rates, identical for all, no quote needed. Speed to implementation: access in 24 hours, data immediately available via portal.

The downside: complete absence of advice and anticipation. The platform records, classifies and files. It does not ask questions, anticipate or arbitrate. If the owner asks about tax optimization, they will get a surface-level chatbot answer, not thoughtful advice.

## Which chartered accounting for which issue?

**For a standard question (monthly accounting, payroll, filings)**: online platform for cost and speed, generalist firm for security and proximity.

**For a structural issue (transfer, financing, complex tax optimization)**: generalist firm or independent firm for long-term relationship and anticipation.

**For a micro-business in cruise mode (few changes, standard questions)**: online platform for cost and transparency.

**For an SME in strong growth or facing transformation**: generalist firm to have a dedicated advisor who understands the whole picture and can arbitrate.

## Why a chartered accounting partner with proximity makes the difference

An SME owner makes on average 20 to 30 decisions each year that impact structure, tax organization or cash flow. On each one, good advice is not applying an accounting rule. It is integrated vision, anticipation, arbitrating between tax, social and financial impacts. From [business creation](/en/blog/accountant-company-formation/) through [tax optimization](/en/blog/accountant-business-tax/) and [electronic invoicing management](/en/blog/accounting-firm-electronic-invoicing/), this partner makes all the difference. For a comprehensive view across all dimensions, see our [complete guide to the best business consulting firms](/en/blog/best-business-consulting-firm/).

A chartered accounting partner who has known the file for several years and understands the company's full structure gains over a transactional approach. It anticipates problems; a platform only records their traces.

This proximity is only achieved if the firm has a local proximity network or a lasting personalized relationship. At In Extenso, 250 offices guarantee that an owner accesses an advisor in their region. At an independent firm, it is personal relationship that creates the link. An online platform creates no proximity, but it does not need it for its target market.
