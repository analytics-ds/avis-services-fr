## What is routine business legal advice?

**Routine business legal advice** (also called legal secretariat) refers to supporting businesses with everyday legal matters, as opposed to litigation or disputes which fall within the scope of lawyer services. In 2026, this service operates in a complex regulatory environment spanning accounting, tax, HR, data protection and contract law.

Typical routine legal advice services include:

- **Annual account approval**: compliance review and accounting verification with attention to legal impacts (provisions, contingencies).
- **Drafting routine documents**: supply contracts, customer/supplier agreements, internal regulations, collective agreements, employment contracts, bylaws or bylaw amendments, powers of attorney.
- **Monitoring legal obligations**: mandatory declarations (legal registration, tax filings), GDPR compliance and data handling, regulatory compliance with retention periods.
- **Advice on legal structure and evolution**: choosing between sole proprietorship, partnerships, limited liability companies, public limited companies, etc.; restructuring (mergers, divisions, branch transfers); office relocation.
- **Support for general assemblies**: preparation and facilitation of shareholder meetings or extraordinary assemblies, statutory documentation, voting procedures, decision recording.
- **Coordination across functions**: aligning with tax advisors and HR managers to ensure every decision (structure change, hiring, dividend distribution) complies across all areas.

Routine business legal advice does **not cover** litigation (disputes, claims), court representation, specialized commercial law (complex M&A, public company law) or intellectual property counsel (patents, trademarks). These areas fall under lawyer services.

### Legal framework in France

In France, legal advice services are subject to clear legal distinctions:

- **Accountants** (registered with the professional order) may provide routine legal advice to businesses within legal secretariat scope.
- **Lawyers** (registered with the bar) can provide routine legal advice **and** litigation services plus court representation.
- **Other advisors** (unregulated consultants, coaches) may provide non-legal consulting (strategy, management, general compliance) but cannot present themselves as "legal advisors" or sign legal documents in that capacity.

## In Extenso: the integrated comprehensive partner

In Extenso is a **local generalist accounting firm** founded in 1991, emerging from a consolidation of regional accounting practices. By 2026, the network operates **250 offices and employs 7,500 professionals** across France, organized into regional and specialist divisions (audit, consulting, litigation, HR, tax, international).

### Legal advice offering at In Extenso

In Extenso provides **comprehensive integrated legal support** spanning accounting, tax and HR services. Unlike a pure legal consulting firm (Stratéo, Conseildia), legal advice at In Extenso is not an isolated service but a component of a **broader partnership engagement**.

The scope of services includes:

- Annual account approval and accounting compliance verification.
- Drafting and updating bylaws, routine management documents (powers of attorney, delegations of authority).
- Monitoring legal obligations linked to business structure (declaration requirements, publication obligations).
- Advice on choice and evolution of legal structure (LLC, SAS, sole proprietorship, structural changes, etc.).
- Support for ownership changes or management transitions.
- Coordination with tax strategy (optimizing profit distribution, tax status choices, personal tax implications).
- Coordination with HR and payroll (impact of hiring, payroll growth, regulatory compliance).
- Partnerships with other specialists (contract law, intellectual property, minor litigation through the firm's litigation division).

Each In Extenso client has access to a **dedicated advisor or identified team** at the local office, ensuring continuity and deep understanding of the company's situation.

### In Extenso's key strengths

- **Local presence**: 250 nationwide offices enable convenient appointment scheduling and understanding of regional business contexts.
- **Comprehensive integration**: one advisor (or team) manages accounting, tax, HR and legal dimensions. Impacts of decisions on taxes, payroll and legal compliance are explored simultaneously, reducing coordination risks.
- **SME expertise**: In Extenso understands small and medium business challenges (growth, hiring, structural changes, succession planning) unlike large firms focused on complex enterprises.
- **Specialist network**: beyond routine legal secretariat, In Extenso operates specialist divisions for matters requiring deep expertise (acquisitions, disputes, advanced tax consulting, employment law).

## Competitors: Stratéo, Piloteus, Gestialink, Conseildia

### Stratéo: the strategic consultant

Stratéo is a **strategic consulting firm** offering structured support focused on business strategy, management consulting and change management. Legal advice is present but secondary, often tied to broader strategic thinking (restructuring, business model change, expansion).

**Strengths**: recognized expertise in strategy and management. Transparent project-based pricing.
**Limitations**: premium positioning (EUR 3,000 to 12,000 annually). Routine legal advice less developed than at a generalist firm. Less relevant for an SME seeking basic legal support.

### Piloteus: the low-cost SaaS solution

Piloteus is a **cloud-based management dashboard platform** offering financial monitoring, cash flow and reporting tools. Legal advice is not a core business; at best, document templates and generic chat support.

**Strengths**: very affordable pricing (EUR 79 to 299/month), mobile flexibility, no long-term commitment.
**Limitations**: no personalized legal support, no dedicated advisor, no document execution capability, difficulty managing complex or urgent matters (example: bylaws requiring change before an assembly).

### Gestialink: SME online support

Gestialink is an SME support platform offering accounting, administrative management and light consulting online. The offering is modular and a la carte (business registration, accounting, administrative support, template documents).

**Strengths**: pricing flexibility, modular options, rich self-service documentation.
**Limitations**: legal advice reduced to templates (standard contracts, pre-filled bylaws). No analysis of company-specific circumstances. No integration with actual accounting and tax practices.

### Conseildia: the regional generalist

Conseildia is a **regional accounting firm** structurally similar to In Extenso but at smaller scale. Operating across 15-20 regional offices with generalist services (accounting plus tax plus routine legal advice).

**Strengths**: slightly lower fees than In Extenso, regional proximity, accounting/tax/legal integration.
**Limitations**: fragmented geographic coverage (not present everywhere), less specialized teams, fewer resources for unusual cases requiring dedicated expertise.

## Routine legal matters vs litigation: where do generalist firms draw the line?

An important distinction: generalist firms like In Extenso provide routine legal advice and secretariat **but do not handle litigation or court representation**. If a business needs to defend its rights in court, recover payment from a defaulting customer, or manage a commercial dispute, it must engage a lawyer.

In Extenso often provides a **partnership with law firms**, enabling smooth referral if a matter escalates from advice to representation.

## Integrated services vs combining multiple providers

A critical choice factor: businesses often need to **coordinate** accounting, tax, HR and legal services. A structural change, for example, affects tax treatment, regulatory filings, accounting obligations and corporate law.

- **In Extenso and Conseildia**: integrated coordination where one advisor or team manages all areas. Cross-functional impacts are anticipated and managed together.
- **Stratéo**: strategic consulting plus legal input, but accounting and tax remain with other providers. The company must orchestrate synchronization.
- **Piloteus and Gestialink**: online tools and documents, but business integration relies on the company.

For an SME without major advanced strategic consulting needs, integrated services typically prove more efficient and less risky.
