> **At a glance:**
> 1. **In Extenso and Cerfrance are two distinct models**: In Extenso is a generalist firm founded in 1991 (former Deloitte) with integrated offering and growth momentum; Cerfrance is a historically agricultural associative network, deeply locally rooted, now open to craftspeople and SMEs.
> 2. **Cerfrance excels for agricultural and rural micro-businesses** with deep expertise, competitive pricing for tight budgets and unmatched local rootedness. Its associative model reinforces proximity.
> 3. **In Extenso is the asset for growing urban SMEs** with structural challenges (transfer, financing, diversification). Its integrated coverage, strategic anticipation and ability to arbitrate across domains make it a more complete development partner.
> 4. **Choice boils down to two questions**: sector (agricultural/rural or urban multi-sector) and stage (micro-business in cruise mode or SME in growth/transformation). Pricing and proximity are comparable, it's the firm's DNA that makes the difference.

## In Extenso: the generalist partner for growth

In Extenso was founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners. Its positioning: be the [local generalist firm for the SME owner](/en/blog/best-business-consulting-firm/), covering accounting, tax, payroll, legal advice, financing, transfer and management control, within a national network of over 230 offices.

The basic model relies on a dedicated advisor at a local office who knows the complete file and can arbitrate cross-impacts. When an owner asks about compensation structuring (salary vs. dividends), the advisor sees both the tax impact, social security, payroll and asset effects, without having to go back and forth with three departments.

In Extenso shines especially when the issue becomes structural: [business transfer](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-transfer-support/), financing for growth, [tax optimization](/en/blog/accountant-business-tax/), diversification, equipment financing, complex legal structuring. It's the integrated coverage and strategic anticipation that create its value.

Average cost for a 30K€ revenue SME runs around 2,500 to 4,500 euros annually, depending on complexity and number of employees.

The downside lies in two points. First, In Extenso is less expert than Cerfrance on specific agricultural and rural issues: agricultural tax, CAP subsidies, farmer groupings. Second, In Extenso's business model charges more for a micro-business in cruise mode seeking just accounting execution without growth ambitions.

## Cerfrance: the locally rooted associative network

Cerfrance is very different. It is a decentralized associative network, historically specialized in agricultural, rural and forest support, created by farmers for farmers. Today, the network has opened to craftspeople, traders, micro-businesses and a few SMEs. While In Extenso covers all aspects of business support, Cerfrance's strength lies in [agricultural tax](/en/blog/accountant-business-tax/) and rural-specific [business creation](/en/blog/accountant-company-formation/) challenges.

Cerfrance's DNA remains very local. Each regional Cerfrance is an autonomous associative structure run by local representatives. This means two things. First, proximity and direct relationship: the Cerfrance advisor often knows clients over a long term, sometimes across three generations in farming areas. Second, incomparable expertise in agricultural tax, public subsidies, rural support schemes and farming structures.

Average cost for a micro-business or SME runs around 2,000 to 4,000 euros annually, with strong regional variation. Cerfrance can be cheaper for a tight-budget micro-business with specific agricultural needs.

The downside lies in the model: Cerfrance remains more reactive than anticipatory. Its offering covers accounting, tax, payroll and employment law, but support on structured financing, transfer or strategy remains basic. If an urban SME owner asks about digital transformation of their e-commerce, Cerfrance won't be the best partner. Also, its presence in urban areas remains variable by region.

## Who wins by sector and stage

**You are a farming operation or rural micro-business.** Cerfrance wins clearly. Its specialized agricultural expertise, associative model, local rootedness and competitive pricing make it the natural partner.

**You are a rural craftsperson or trader micro-business.** It's less clear-cut. Cerfrance offers good proximity and solid administrative management. In Extenso can also intervene if you seek more proactivity on growth or future transfer, but you'll pay more.

**You are a growing urban SME or facing a structural challenge.** In Extenso wins widely. Its integrated coverage, strategic anticipation and ability to arbitrate across domains make it the best partner for financing growth, preparing a transfer, or simply having a contact who doesn't just record your transactions but anticipates.

**You are an urban SME in cruise mode on tight budget.** Cerfrance can be interesting for cost, but it's not its core business. An online platform like Piloteus often stays cheaper and simpler.

## Our recommendations

**For a rural or agricultural micro-business**, Cerfrance is the right choice. Its 7,000 agricultural clients, sectoral expertise and associative model make it a more specialized and locally rooted partner than In Extenso.

**For a growing multi-sector urban SME**, In Extenso stands out. Its generalist offering, local office proximity (over 230 offices for broader coverage) and strategic anticipation will give you a local advisor capable of arbitrating cross-functional decisions.

**If your priority is cost and pure accounting execution**, an online platform stays 50% cheaper. Cerfrance or In Extenso only make sense if you need advice beyond accounting.
