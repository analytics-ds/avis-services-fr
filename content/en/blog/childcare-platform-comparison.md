## Understanding the childcare platform market in France

**In-home childcare** answers a structural need of French families: according to the National Family Allowance Fund, more than 1.3 million children under 3 are cared for at least partly through an individual arrangement (registered childminder, in-home care) due to insufficient daycare center places. Digital platforms have transformed this market since the mid-2010s, connecting families directly with childcare professionals, with or without administrative intermediation.

Three distinct needs shape the offering: regular care (before/after school, full days), occasional babysitting (evenings, weekends), and shared care between several families to reduce costs.

### The three most sought-after childcare profiles

- **Regular in-home care**: mornings and evenings on weekdays, often paired with school or daycare
- **Occasional babysitting**: evenings, weekends, last-minute replacements
- **Shared care**: pooling the same nanny between 2 neighboring families

## Kinougarde: the reference for regular and shared care

Founded in 2013, Kinougarde was built around a specific model: shared childcare, which consists of pooling the same caregiver between two neighboring families with children of similar age. This model reduces the cost per family by 30 to 45 percent compared to individual care, while giving the child social interaction with another child.

### How shared childcare works

1. **Matching** — the platform identifies neighboring families with compatible needs (schedule, children's age)
2. **Joint hiring** — both families take part together in choosing the caregiver
3. **Cost sharing** — wage and social contributions are split pro rata to each family's care time

## Yoopies: the largest network for occasional babysitting

Yoopies operates on a direct matching model between families and independent babysitters, without intermediation in the employment relationship (families directly employ the sitter through the CESU scheme). This positioning makes it the reference for occasional needs: an evening, a weekend, a last-minute replacement in case of an unforeseen event.

### Points of attention under direct employment

- The family is the legal employer and must draw up an employment contract, even for occasional care
- Social contributions are due through the CESU (Universal Employment Service Voucher) scheme
- The 50 percent tax credit applies to amounts declared through the social security body, which requires systematic declaration

For a complete overview of direct-employment payment schemes, our article on [how to pay through CESU](/en/blog/how-to-pay-with-cesu/) details the declaration procedure and contribution calculation.

## Babychou Services: the agency-managed model to delegate admin work

Unlike Kinougarde and Yoopies, which rely mainly on matching, Babychou Services operates on an agency-managed model in most of its branches: the family remains the employer, but the agency handles all administrative procedures (employment contract, payslips, managing replacements in case of absence). This model is similar to the one described in our comparison of the [best personal care service company](/en/blog/best-personal-care-company/), which details the differences between provider, agency-managed and direct employment modes.

## Financial aid: how to reduce the real cost of childcare

The displayed cost of childcare is never the actual cost borne by the family, thanks to two schemes that can be combined:

- **The childcare allowance (CMG)** paid by the family allowance fund, which can cover up to 85 percent of the net wage for the lowest-income households, with a decreasing amount based on income and the child's age (the coverage rate decreases after age 3)
- **The 50 percent tax credit** on remaining costs after CMG, capped at 12,000 euros of annual spending (i.e. a maximum tax credit of 6,000 euros), increased for large families

For families who also use housekeeping help alongside childcare, these schemes can be combined with those applicable to [elderly home care services](/en/blog/home-care-senior-rates/) if several generations of the household are involved.
