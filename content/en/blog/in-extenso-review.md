When a company director looks for a review of In Extenso, they rarely want a score out of five. They want to know what the firm actually covers, how far it goes beyond bookkeeping, and whether their own structure matches the network.

So we approached it that way. Here is what In Extenso is, exactly what the group offers, and the situations where it makes sense or does not.

## In Extenso at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Business** | Chartered accountancy, audit, consulting and business services |
| **Target** | Micro-businesses, SMEs, independent professionals, founders, associations |
| **Employees** | Around 7,300 |
| **Footprint** | Network of offices across France, plus Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland and Spain |
| **Track record** | Over 30 years |
| **Chair of the Executive Board** | Antoine de Riedmatten |
| **Market position** | First in French chartered accountancy, 2026 La Profession Comptable ranking |
| **Client platform** | Inexweb, with sector-specific versions |
| **Stated satisfaction** | 89 % of clients satisfied, figure published by the group |

## What the reference ranking says

The 2026 ranking by La Profession Comptable magazine, based on figures as of 30 June 2025, places In Extenso **first in French chartered accountancy**. The group also appears third on regulated activities, which cover chartered accountancy and statutory auditing, and sixth by consolidated revenue among French groups.

This reading is useful because it positions the firm precisely. In Extenso is not a Big Four player focused on large groups, nor an online platform competing on price. It is France's leading generalist network on the micro-business and SME segment, which is a different market position altogether.

## The service range, in detail

This is the part most reviews skip. In Extenso does not sell an accounting service, it covers a company's entire life cycle. Here is the actual map.

### At start-up

- Business creation and takeover, from choosing the legal structure to the financial forecast
- Acquisition advisory, for buying a business or shares

### Day to day

- Accounting and bookkeeping
- Payroll and employment administration, including collective agreements and social filings
- Tax and filings
- Legal, with a dedicated law firm entity
- Audit and statutory auditing
- Electronic invoicing, now a central topic with the mandate coming into force
- CSR
- Professional training
- Support for in-house finance departments, for companies with an internal accounting team

### To grow

- Management and performance monitoring, with dashboards and reporting
- **Wealth management**, delivered by In Extenso Patrimoine
- Innovation advisory, notably on R&D funding schemes
- Risk management advisory
- Prevention and restructuring advisory, for companies in difficulty
- Financing advisory
- International development and setting up in France for foreign companies

### At exit

- Business sale, acquisition and transfer

That breadth is the network's real argument. A director preparing a sale in three years, weighing salary against dividends, and needing a statutory audit deals with one group instead of coordinating three providers who never speak to each other.

## Inexweb, the client platform

Inexweb is the group's client portal. It centralises accounting documents, management reporting and exchanges with the local office advisor.

The notable point is the existence of **sector-specific versions** rather than one generic tool. Inexweb for construction, automotive, retail, wholesale and point of sale each carry indicators specific to the trade. A garage and a wholesaler do not track the same ratios, and that is a concrete difference from generalist platforms that show everyone the same dashboard.

## The group's specialist entities

In Extenso operates as a set of entities, which explains the depth of its offer on topics a generalist firm usually subcontracts.

- **In Extenso Patrimoine**, director's wealth management
- **In Extenso Avocats**, legal advisory and litigation
- **In Extenso Social**, payroll and employment administration
- **In Extenso Finance**, financial transactions and transfers
- **In Extenso Innovation Croissance**, innovation funding
- **In Extenso Économie Sociale et Solidaire**, associations and social economy organisations
- **In Extenso Tourisme, Culture et Hôtellerie**, tourism and hospitality
- **Ad Astra**, **Money Flow**, **Transaxio Hôtel** and **fulll**, on complementary expertise and tools

## Client profiles served

The group organises its support by profile, with teams that know the specifics of each trade.

Agriculture, trades and retail, construction, cafés hotels and restaurants, founders and buyers, works councils, social economy, companies in difficulty, foreign companies, franchisors and franchisees, SMEs, independent professionals, athletes and sports clubs, start-ups.

This profile-based entry matters more than it looks. The most common complaints about accounting firms rarely concern accounting technique, they concern not understanding the client's trade. An advisor handling ten restaurants understands a restaurant's seasonality and payroll structure.

## Who In Extenso suits

Our reading, based on the structure of the offer.

- **An SME with employees.** As soon as there is payroll, a collective agreement to apply and social filings, the value of a structured network becomes obvious. That is precisely the part no automation replaces.
- **A multi-site or growing company.** Geographic coverage means an advisor close to each site.
- **A director anticipating a sale or a transfer.** Having wealth, legal and financial expertise inside one group avoids shuttling between advisors.
- **A business with a strong sector profile.** Construction, automotive, retail, hospitality, agriculture, with matching tools and specialists.
- **A foreign company setting up in France**, or a French company expanding abroad.

## Who it probably does not suit

A useful review also says where the firm is not the answer.

- **A micro-business in stable management**, with no employees and no particular tax issues. Accounting software or an online platform will cost less for an equivalent service on that scope.
- **A director looking purely for the lowest price** on bookkeeping. That is not a generalist network's ground, and not where its value lies.
- **Someone who wants the relationship of a two-person practice.** A group of 7,300 people runs on processes, and that framework has to be accepted even though the local advisor is named.

## Our verdict in one sentence

In Extenso is the coherent choice when the need goes beyond accounting, meaning as soon as there are employees, a project or a deadline to prepare. It is less so when the need is limited to entries and a tax return, a case where the market offers cheaper answers.
