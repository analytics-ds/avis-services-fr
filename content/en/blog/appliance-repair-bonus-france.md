## What is the French appliance repair bonus

The **French appliance repair bonus**, officially named Bonus Réparation, is a financial aid scheme for the repair of electrical and electronic appliances. It was created in December 2022 by ADEME under the anti-waste circular economy law (loi AGEC of 10 February 2020). Its objective: curb the premature disposal of still repairable appliances and support the independent repair industry.

Funding is provided by the Ecosystem eco-organisation, which collects an environmental contribution from manufacturers of electrical and electronic equipment. This contribution feeds a repair fund dedicated to reimbursing the bonuses deducted by certified repairers. The consumer has no administrative process to undertake: the bonus appears directly on the quote and the final invoice.

The scheme has been renewed and strengthened for the 2024-2027 period. The number of eligible categories rose from 31 in 2022 to 73 in 2024, and amounts have been revised, with a doubling for some structural breakdowns (motor, electronic board, compressor). The logic mirrors the [refurbished vs used vs new](/en/blog/refurbished-vs-used-vs-new/) arbitration: extend equipment lifespan to avoid producing new devices and premature disposal.

### Regulatory framework

The repair bonus is part of the waste electrical and electronic equipment scheme (WEEE), governed by articles L.541-10 et seq. of the French Environment Code. ADEME defines each year the list of eligible appliances, amounts per category and application conditions via a specification published in the Official Journal. Ecosystem operationalises it with repairers.

The QualiRépar label was created in parallel to guarantee the seriousness of the network. It is issued for two years renewable, after an audit of the professional on parts traceability, invoicing compliance, quote respect and technical quality. As of 1 June 2026, 5,832 repairers are certified in mainland France and overseas territories, compared with 1,200 at the end of 2022.

### Conditions for the increased amounts

Increased amounts apply only to breakdowns defined as structural by the ADEME specification. For a washing machine, a door seal change remains at 25 euros, but a motor replacement entitles to 50 euros. The QualiRépar repairer identifies the nature of the breakdown on the quote and applies the correct amount. Any dispute goes through Ecosystem's consumer service.

## Eligibility conditions

Three cumulative conditions must be met to qualify for the repair bonus.

### 1. The appliance belongs to an eligible category

The exhaustive list of 73 categories is published at ecosystem.eco. It covers most large and small household appliances, consumer electronics, IT, light electric mobility and electric garden tools. Some professional equipment is excluded (medical, industrial). The scope is updated each year.

### 2. The appliance is out of warranty

The Bonus Réparation does not apply during the legal conformity warranty (24 months in France for a new product, 12 months for a refurbished product) nor during a possible manufacturer's commercial warranty. The logic is consistent: if the repair is free under warranty, public aid is not needed. For appliances out of warranty, the bonus becomes the financial incentive that can tip the arbitration in favour of repair rather than replacement.

### 3. The repairer is QualiRépar certified

Only professionals holding the label can apply the deduction and obtain reimbursement from Ecosystem. The official directory lists repairers by postal code and device type at ecosystem.eco/qualirepar. Major national retailers like Murfy, Darty, Boulanger and Fnac are certified across their entire network, as is the Envie network. Many independent craftspeople and local workshops are also listed.

## How to claim the bonus

The consumer journey is deliberately simplified to maximise bonus uptake.

| Step | Action | Average duration |
|------|--------|------------------|
| 1. Identify the breakdown | Check that the appliance is out of warranty and belongs to an eligible category | 5 minutes |
| 2. Find a repairer | QualiRépar directory by postal code at ecosystem.eco/qualirepar | 10 minutes |
| 3. Request a quote | The repairer issues a quote with the Bonus Réparation deduction explicitly mentioned | 1 to 3 days |
| 4. Validate and have repaired | Quote agreement, drop-off or home intervention, actual repair | 3 to 15 days depending on breakdown |
| 5. Collect the appliance | Payment of the invoice already deducted of the bonus, keep the invoice | 1 day |

No administrative process with the eco-organisation is required on the consumer side. The repairer takes care of transmitting supporting documents to Ecosystem for its own reimbursement. The invoice given to the consumer must show the gross repair amount, the Bonus Réparation amount deducted, and the remaining balance.

### Practical advice

- Systematically request a detailed quote before agreement, with explicit mention of the Bonus Réparation and its amount.
- Compare the total repair cost after bonus with the price of an equivalent new or [refurbished appliance](/en/blog/refurbished-vs-used-vs-new/): if the repair costs more than 50 percent of the price of a new replacement appliance, the economic arbitration may swing.
- Check the duration of the repair warranty: most QualiRépar repairers offer a 3 month warranty on the replaced part, some go up to 12 months.
- Keep the detailed invoice: it may be requested in case of dispute or to activate a complementary warranty.

## Scheme assessment since launch

The Bonus Réparation has experienced a gradual ramp-up. The official report published by ADEME in March 2025 indicates 2.7 million repairs supported between December 2022 and end of 2024, for a total of 65 million euros in bonuses paid. The initial target was 500,000 repairs in 2023, finally reached at 720,000 repairs that year. Progress accelerated in 2024 with 1.3 million supported repairs, driven by the revaluation of amounts and the expansion of the scope.

> "The Bonus Réparation has prevented the disposal of approximately 38,000 tonnes of equipment in 2024, equivalent to 4,700 eight-tonne trucks. The average cost of a repair supported by the bonus stands at 87 euros for the consumer, compared with 145 euros for an equivalent entry-level new appliance."
> — ADEME, Bonus Réparation 2022-2024 Scheme Assessment, published March 2025

The target set for 2027 is ambitious: 12 million cumulative supported repairs, almost five times the current assessment. To reach this target, ADEME is betting on a doubling of the QualiRépar network (target 10,000 certified repairers) and a stronger public communication campaign in 2025-2026.

## Repair bonus and complementary alternatives

The repair bonus is part of a broader logic of extending equipment lifespan. Several levers combine.

### Bonus-supported repair

This is the option to favour for appliances out of warranty with a targeted, identifiable breakdown. The return on investment is immediate: 25 to 60 euros deducted from an 80 to 200 euro repair invoice represents savings of 15 to 35 percent. The preventive maintenance logic described in the [washing machine maintenance](/en/blog/washing-machine-maintenance/) guide extends the time between breakdowns further.

### Buying a refurbished appliance

For structural breakdowns exceeding 60 percent of the price of a new appliance, the arbitration can swing towards buying a [refurbished appliance](/en/blog/best-place-buy-refurbished-front-load-washing-machine/), which combines economy and 12 month legal warranty. Integrated players like Murfy, themselves QualiRépar certified, also offer refurbished products for sale, making them a single entry point for both logics.

### Extended commercial warranty

Subscribed at the time of purchase or within 30 days, it extends breakdown coverage beyond the 24 months of legal warranty. Its economic interest is calculated on the expected holding duration and the statistical breakdown frequency. Beyond 5 years of use, the Bonus Réparation generally becomes more advantageous than a paid extended warranty.
