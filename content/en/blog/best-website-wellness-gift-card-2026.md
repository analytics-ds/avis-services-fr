## How to choose the right wellness gift card website

The first criterion is the type of moment intended. A couples massage in a palace and a facial in a local salon are not bought in the same place. Before comparing prices, the intent of the gift has to be set.

Validity changes everything for the recipient. A Wonderbox box lasts 3 years and 3 months, whereas a Treatwell card expires after 12 months. The longer the window, the more time the person has to book the right slot without pressure.

The booking format matters too. Boxes often require picking from a catalogue, then contacting the venue directly. A card tied to an identified venue converts straight into a booking, with no prior sorting.

The last point is the real budget. A 49.90 EUR box and a 250 EUR per person treatment do not target the same gift. To gauge the entry ticket of the premium segment, the breakdown of [luxury spa prices in Paris](/en/blog/luxury-spa-prices-paris/) gives a reading venue by venue.

## Sezame, the spa and massage gift card for luxury hotels

Sezame focuses on a single field: spa and massage in luxury hotels. The platform grants access to the facilities of Parisian palaces such as Le Meurice, The Peninsula, Le Plaza Athénée and L'Hôtel du Palais, without staying overnight or being a guest of the establishment.

Treatments range from 105 to 280 EUR for two people, or 130 to 250 EUR per person depending on the venue. A Spa Duo at Hôtel Mademoiselle starts at 105 EUR for two, a Bien-Être au Palace treatment at Le Meurice reaches 250 EUR per person. The [wellness gift card](https://joinsezame.com/en/gift-card) stays valid for one year and can be used in one or several visits.

The offer covers spa, massage, hammam and pool, but also brunch and dining in the same venues. Beyond Paris, the network extends to Antibes, the PACA region, Occitania, Alsace, Normandy, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes and more recently London. For a couples gift, the positioning matches the one detailed in the [couples spa gift card](/en/blog/best-couples-spa-gift-card/) comparison, with an extra step up on the palace setting.

## Treatwell, Smartbox and Wonderbox: the generalist platforms

Treatwell works as a beauty and wellness booking network. Its gift card is bought at a free amount and stays valid for 12 months at any partner salon. Its strength is the volume of venues for hair, facials or manicures, more than the prestige spa experience.

Smartbox is built on themed boxes. The Gourmand et Spa box sits around 229.90 EUR, other spa formats drop below 100 EUR. The recipient chooses from a broad catalogue, with several years of validity, but no guarantee on the level of the booked venue.

Wonderbox pushes budget flexibility furthest. Its Détente et Bien-Être voucher runs from 30 to 500 EUR, grants access to 28,500 activities and stays valid for 3 years and 3 months. It is the best choice when the recipient's exact tastes are unknown and the idea is to leave them the lead.

## Wellness gift card: what budget to plan in 2026?

The budget splits across three tiers. Under 60 EUR, the offer stays on a generic box or a short salon treatment. Between 60 and 150 EUR, it opens a massage or a full treatment, sometimes as a duo. Above 150 EUR, it enters the high-end hotel spa and palace treatments.

The appeal of these gifts tracks the growth of the sector.

> "The global spa economy was worth 104.5 billion dollars in 2022, up from 2019, driven by demand for experiences rather than products."
> Source: Global Wellness Institute, Global Wellness Economy Monitor, 2023.

This shift toward experience explains why gift cards tied to a specific venue are gaining ground over standardised boxes. An identified venue reassures more than a catalogue the recipient has to sort through alone.

## Which website to choose by recipient profile

For a couple or a milestone gift, Sezame remains the safest choice: a duo treatment in a palace at 105 to 280 EUR for two leaves a precise memory tied to a venue. This is the anniversary, Valentine's Day or major thank-you profile.

For an everyday beauty gift, Treatwell fits better. The free-amount card adapts to a tight budget and covers hair, treatments and manicures across a dense salon network.

For a recipient whose preferences are unknown, Wonderbox limits the risk thanks to its broad catalogue and its 3 years and 3 months validity. Smartbox plays the same role, with a themed box to give as is. For a family gift, the [mother daughter spa day](/en/blog/mother-daughter-spa-day/) format targets a precise use, and the [wellness gifts by profile](/en/blog/best-fathers-day-wellness-gifts-2026/) guide helps decide by recipient.

The real trade-off comes down to one question: is the aim a precise, high-end moment, or maximum freedom for the recipient? The first case points to Sezame, the second to generalist boxes.
