## What exactly is a handyman

The **handyman** profession (sometimes called "all-rounder" or "odd-job man") covers versatile professionals who intervene on unregulated small jobs at home. The profession is not a regulated profession in France: no specific diploma is required, provided they do not encroach on reserved domains (gas, embedded electrical, pressurized plumbing, building structure).

The most common French business code for this profession is 81.21Z (general building cleaning) or 96.09Z (other personal services). Depending on status, the handyman can be self-employed (revenue cap at 77,700 euros in 2026 for services), micro-business, or salaried at a personal services company.

### List of typical handyman jobs

The standard intervention scope covers:

- Flat-pack furniture assembly (IKEA, Maisons du Monde, Conforama, La Redoute).
- Wall fixing: paintings, mirrors, shelves, TVs, wall brackets.
- Curtain rod and blind installation.
- Minor plumbing: seal replacement, mixers, toilet flush mechanism, simple unclogging.
- Minor electrical work: replacing outlets, switches, light fixtures (without working on the panel).
- Touch-up painting and small painting jobs (bedroom, hallway).
- Repair tiling, silicone jointing.
- Light gardening: mowing, weeding, hedge trimming, leaf collection.
- Small interior moving, furniture assembly and disassembly.
- Furniture repair (hinges, handles, legs).

What a handyman **cannot do** without specific qualification:

- Gas circuit installation or modification.
- Embedded electrical installation or panel modification.
- Pressurized plumbing (network connection, boiler).
- Structural work (load-bearing walls, framework).
- Roofing or exterior joinery installation.

## How to choose a reliable handyman

Four criteria structure the choice of a reliable provider.

### Criterion 1: insurance and legal status

Always check the existence of professional liability insurance (RC pro). In case of damage (broken tile, incorrectly drilled wall), this insurance covers. Ask for a certificate less than 12 months old. Also verify legal status (sole trader, SARL, SAS) via the SIRET number on infogreffe.fr.

### Criterion 2: reviews and ratings

On platforms (AlloVoisins, Yoojo, Frizbiz), only retain providers with at least 20 reviews and an average score above 4.5 out of 5. Read detailed reviews, not just the average score. Reviews mentioning respect for deadlines and site cleanliness are more reliable than those mentioning only friendliness.

### Criterion 3: written quote before intervention

For any mission above 100 euros, require a detailed written quote (labor, travel, materials). This quote is legally mandatory and binds the provider. Compare 2 to 3 quotes to avoid unjustified price differences.

### Criterion 4: personal services tax credit

To benefit from the 50 percent tax credit (cap of 500 euros per year for small handyman work), the provider must be certified for personal services and the mission must last less than 2 consecutive hours. Check certification on nova.gouv.fr.

## Practical tips before hiring

Five vigilance points help secure an intervention. To organize more broadly with peer-to-peer services, the guide [best peer-to-peer service platform](/en/blog/best-peer-to-peer-service-platform/) compares matchmaking platforms.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Paying in cash without invoice: no guarantee in case of damage, no tax credit possible.
2. Entrusting regulated work (gas, embedded electrical) to an unqualified handyman: risk of legal liability in case of incident.
3. Choosing solely on lowest price: difference often linked to lack of insurance or undeclared work.
4. Not checking the SIRET: potential sign of undeclared work.
5. Accepting a mission without a written quote above 100 euros: guaranteed surprise overcharges.
