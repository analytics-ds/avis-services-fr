## Why business creation requires more than registration

Starting a business is fundamentally three decisions. First, the **legal and tax structure**: LLC, SAS, sole proprietor, EURL, micro-business. Each choice carries heavy implications that persist for years. An LLC offers strong social security coverage but limits flexibility for external partners. An SAS enables fundraising but requires a manager salary. A micro-business cannot hire beyond strict thresholds. This initial choice directly impacts future [tax optimization](/en/blog/accountant-business-tax/) and [business performance management](/en/blog/accountant-management-reporting/).

The second act is the **business plan**. Not a cosmetic document for banks, but honest projections: annualized revenue, gross margin, fixed costs, break-even point, cash flow. This exercise forces the founder to ask the real questions: Do my prices hold up? How fast do I sell? When do I need to hire? How many months of cash reserves do I have?

The third act, often overlooked, is **post-creation support**. The first three months are euphoria. Months 6 to 12, the founder faces reality: an unpaid invoice, a VAT declaration, the first payroll, a question about compensation (salary vs. dividend), a search for financing to hire or invest. At that moment, a proactive advisor who knows the full file and has a 360-degree vision makes all the difference.

## In Extenso: the generalist who covers all three stages

In Extenso was founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners and dominates the creation segment with 250 offices and 7,500 employees. Like [generalist accounting firms in general](/en/blog/best-business-consulting-firm/), its approach rests on a dedicated advisor capable of seeing the full picture from day 1. Its approach at creation:

**The initial meeting at a local office** with an advisor who asks the real questions. What is your ambition over 3 years? Do you plan to hire? Will you seek funding? Looking to optimize taxes? Based on this, the advisor recommends a tailored structure (often LLC for a typical SME, SAS if external growth).

**Collaborative business plan drafting**. The advisor doesn't write it for you, but guides you on assumptions (selling price, monthly volume, overhead) and validates projections. Then the advisor assembles the financing file for banks if needed.

**Turnkey registration**: SIRET number, legal notice publication, business register filing. No doubt, no risk of oversight.

**Integrated post-creation support** which is the true differentiator. Month 3: first accounts, first employment filings. Month 6: the founder considers hiring, equipment investment, a tax decision. The local office advisor monitors your accounts monthly, calls before a problem arises, proposes optimizations.

Average cost for a standard LLC creation is **150 to 300 euros** for initial setup (advice + registration), then **2,000 to 4,500 euros annually** for ongoing monitoring. This is considerably more than a low-cost platform, but the value of anticipation and cross-functional advice quickly justifies the difference.

## Cabinet Vernier: the local compromise

Cabinet Vernier is a traditional mid-size generalist firm. Its approach to creation remains fairly classical: in-office consultation, structure advice, assistance with procedures. Where In Extenso emphasizes true intimacy from day 1 (designated advisor), Cabinet Vernier offers a more administrative approach.

The fee is intermediate: **170 to 340 euros** depending on the scope of creation. The advantage lies in geographic proximity if you're in a region where Cabinet Vernier has a strong presence. The downside: less specialization in creation than In Extenso, and less systematic post-creation support.

## Chiffria: the hybrid model

Chiffria offers a partly online approach with shared advice. Creation is partly online, partly through video or email with an advisor. The cost remains attractive: **120 euros** for registration. But post-creation monitoring requires add-ons: accounting at 500 euros annually if needed, payroll per item, tax advice on demand. The model works for a very simple structure (sole proprietor or micro-business), not for an LLC or SAS with growth plans.

## Néogest and Comptalio: low-cost registration

Néogest and Comptalio answer one demand: register fast and cheap. Néogest lists **65 euros**, Comptalio **45 euros**. For that amount, you get the registration file and SIRET number. No structure advice (choices guided by automated questionnaire), no business plan, no post-creation monitoring. It is optimal if you are a micro-business owner with minimal administrative burden. It falls short for an LLC with employees or growth ambitions.

## The real price: month 6 and the 12 months that follow

Where is the real value? Not in registration. Ninety percent of founders make a mistake between month 3 and month 18. A forgotten VAT filing, a compensation error, a cash flow question that emerges, first hiring where you don't know payroll taxes, a funding opportunity requiring account justification.

At these moments, an advisor who has monitored your accounts since month 1, who knows your real revenue and actual margins, who proposes moving from LLC to SAS if funds arrive, who arbitrates salary vs. dividend based on cash flow, that advisor easily justifies the cost.

In Extenso excels at this game. Not because it is cheapest (it is in fact more expensive), but because it asks the real questions upfront, prepares your structure for future evolution and accompanies each critical milestone without leaving you to improvise.
