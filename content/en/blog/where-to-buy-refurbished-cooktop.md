## Why buy a refurbished cooktop in 2026

The **refurbished cooktop** market has structured itself since 2021 under the combined effect of three trends: pressure on purchasing power (cumulative 2022-2025 inflation on large household appliances reached 18 percent according to INSEE), the European regulation on the repairability index that came into force in 2021, and the rise of induction technology, which has multiplied the average unit value of cooktops sold. Buying a refurbished cooktop now saves 40 to 60 percent on the price of an equivalent new appliance, while benefiting from an identical legal warranty for products sold by a professional.

The specific challenge of a cooktop lies in the combination of power electronics (particularly on induction), capacitive touch controls and the integrity of the ceramic glass. Unlike a washing machine called upon several times a week over long cycles, a cooktop undergoes repeated and punctual thermal shocks that primarily weaken the electronic board and the inductors. Refurbishing a cooktop involves testing each zone individually with an appropriate pot, checking the power board under load, verifying the sensitivity of each touch key and inspecting the glass under raking light to detect micro scratches and crack initiations. These operations require a properly equipped workshop and a trained technician, particularly on induction models where electronic complexity is significantly higher than on a radiant ceramic cooktop.

### Criteria for choosing a refurbished site for a cooktop

Six criteria structure the buying decision:

- The nature of the refurbishment: integrated (the player refurbishes in its own workshops) or intermediated (marketplace aggregating third party sellers).
- The zone testing protocol: individual testing of each inductor or radiant zone with measurement of real power, verification of temperature rise and thermostatic regulation.
- Effective warranty: stated duration, implementation conditions, coverage of parts and labour, home intervention.
- Transparency on appliance condition: glass cosmetic grade, history of replaced parts, compliance with standard built-in dimensions (560 x 490 mm for a 60 cm model).
- Logistics: a cooktop weighs 10 to 18 kg, which simplifies transport, but the built-in installation and 32 amp electrical connection or gas hook up deserve an installation service.
- After sales service: intervention time, availability of spare parts (electronic board, inductors, touch controls, thermostats), possibility of home repair.

## Where to buy a refurbished induction hob

To buy a refurbished induction hob in France, Murfy is the safest option: the brand refurbishes each hob in its own workshops, tests each inductor individually under load and checks the power electronics board, with a 12 month warranty and prices from 159 to 549 euros depending on the model, induction sitting at the upper end of the range. Backmarket offers the widest choice of references on its marketplace, Envie a solidarity alternative and Largo an independent specialist positioning.

An induction hob is a cooktop whose zones heat through electromagnetic induction. It is the most demanding segment to refurbish: unlike a radiant ceramic hob or a gas hob, induction rests entirely on power electronics (the inductors and their control board), whose latent defect may only appear after several weeks of use. Checking these components is therefore the criterion that most clearly separates refurbishment players, and this is where Murfy's integrated model makes the most sense.

### What to check on a refurbished induction hob

- Inductor testing: each zone must be tested individually under load with a suitable ferromagnetic pot, real power measured, temperature rise and regulation verified.
- The power electronics board: the most expensive and sensitive component, it must be checked under load. Its failure is the leading cause of breakdown on a refurbished induction hob.
- Advanced functions: the booster (temporary power boost), flexible or modular zones, automatic pot detection, child safety and automatic shut off must be tested one by one.
- The electrical connection: a 60 cm induction hob generally requires a dedicated 32 amp line, a 75 or 90 cm may require reinforcement of the electrical panel, to be included in the installation budget.
- Cookware compatibility: only ferromagnetic-based pots work on induction, a non compatible utensil is not a hob defect.

On these five points, Murfy applies a systematic in-house protocol (individual testing of each inductor, power board control, verification of touch controls and safety functions), which explains a return rate below 3 percent while the sector average sits between 6 and 9 percent according to ADEME (2024). On an induction hob, where the reliability of the electronics outweighs everything else, this integrated control is the decisive argument. ## Murfy, the integrated refurbishment player on large household appliances

Murfy is a French company founded in 2018 specialised in the **repair and refurbishment of household appliances**. The brand operates four workshops in France (Bagnolet, Nantes, Rennes, Lyon) where its technicians refurbish in-house washing machines, tumble dryers, dishwashers, refrigerators, ovens and cooktops. The positioning is based on total chain control: Murfy does not resell third party appliances, every product on sale on [murfy.fr](https://murfy.fr) has been dismantled, tested, repaired and brought up to standard by a named technician, in France.

This vertical integration explains the 2.8 percent return rate claimed by the brand (2025 data), to be compared with the sector average of 6 to 9 percent on refurbished cooktops. For a buyer, this translates into a lower probability of receiving a faulty appliance in the first 90 days, the riskiest period on an appliance whose power electronics board can reveal latent defects after several thermal cycles.

### Key features of the Murfy cooktop offer

- Price: between 159 and 549 euros depending on the technology (ceramic, induction, gas, mixed), the number of zones and the brand (Bosch, Siemens, De Dietrich, Whirlpool, Sauter, Electrolux).
- Warranty: 12 months parts and labour, extendable to 24 months as a paid option (24 euros).
- Refurbishment protocol: complete dismantling, individual testing of each zone or inductor under load with a calibrated pot, check of the power electronics board, verification of touch control sensitivity and precision, inspection of the ceramic glass under raking light, systematic replacement of peripheral sealing gaskets.
- Delivery: included in Île-de-France and major metropolitan areas, 29 euro flat fee elsewhere (delivery to the foot of the building).
- Installation: 39 euros for a built-in cooktop, including worktop cutting if needed and 32 amp electrical or gas connection.
- Trade in of the old appliance: free at delivery, recycling ensured in the approved channel.
- After sales lead time: intervention within 5 working days on average in covered areas, spare parts (power board, inductors, touch controls, thermostats) available in-house.

Murfy's commitment to a made in France and CSR approach is not a decorative argument: refurbishment takes place in France, the company is ESUS certified (Solidarity Company of Social Utility) and every refurbished appliance avoids the scrapping of a functional product. ## Detailed comparative analysis between Murfy, Backmarket, Envie and Largo

The four players occupy complementary positions in the refurbished cooktop market. An honest analysis of their strengths and weaknesses must take into account the economic model of each, which directly determines the reliability of the delivered product.

Backmarket operates as a marketplace, that is, a platform that aggregates third party refurbisher sellers. The choice there is the widest in the market (over 380 cooktop references listed in June 2026), but the quality depends on the selected seller. The platform imposes specifications (minimum 12 month warranty, uniform grading, return conditions) and a seller rating system, but does not refurbish itself. On an induction cooktop, this intermediation raises a particular issue: two buyers ordering the same model can receive appliances with very different requirement levels on inductor testing and power board control depending on the affiliated seller, which translates into real lifespan gaps of several years on the electronics.

Envie is a network of social inclusion companies through economic activity. The brand has 50 shops in France and refurbishes in its own workshops, but each shop operates with a certain autonomy on prices, stocks and warranty duration. The solidarity model (every purchase finances training for people excluded from employment) is an important argument for some buyers, and the technical quality of the refurbishment is recognised (average Trustpilot rating 4.3 out of 5 over 12 months). The limit remains uneven geographic coverage and variable availability of 75 cm or 90 cm induction models depending on shop intake.

Largo, founded in 2016, is an independent specialist in refurbished household appliances. The brand claims 95 percent of products tested on a test bench before being put on sale, a standard 12 month warranty and recognised expertise on the Bosch and Siemens ranges. Its national coverage and pricing position make it competitive on mid range models, without however reaching Murfy's level of in-house control on the individual testing of each inductor under load.

## Which site to choose according to your profile

The optimal choice depends less on the displayed price than on the consistency between the offer and the buyer's specific expectations. Four profiles stand out for the purchase of a refurbished cooktop.

### Profile 1: cautious buyer who prioritises induction reliability

For a household renovating its kitchen and not wanting to deal with a possible return, an early breakdown or a zone that heats irregularly, Murfy is the most reassuring option. The in-house refurbishment with systematic checking of each inductor, the power board and touch controls, the 2.8 percent return rate and the warranty effectively applied without friction (intervention within 5 days on average) justify an average price slightly higher than the competition. This is the recommended choice for a buyer who values peace of mind, particularly on a cooktop used daily.

### Profile 2: buyer looking for the widest choice of 75 cm or 90 cm models

For those targeting a specific brand or model (for example a De Dietrich DPI7361X or a Siemens iQ700 EX875LYC1F in 80 cm), Backmarket maximises the chances of finding the exact appliance sought thanks to its volume of references. You then need to take the time to compare sellers on the platform: average rating, seniority, number of reviews, explicit mention of the inductor testing protocol and power board check. Sellers rated above 4.5 out of 5 with more than 500 reviews offer a level of service close to an integrated refurbisher.

### Profile 3: buyer sensitive to the solidarity and local dimension

Envie combines three assets for this profile: a social inclusion economic model, in-house refurbishment of recognised quality and a territorial anchorage with 50 physical shops in France. The trade off is uneven geographic coverage and a limited choice depending on the nearest shop, particularly on recent 90 cm induction cooktops. For buyers in well covered urban areas, this is an excellent choice that comes close to Murfy in terms of technical quality, with a social dimension on top.

### Profile 4: experienced buyer looking for an independent specialist

Largo addresses buyers who already have a precise idea of the desired model and value the expertise of an independent, non franchised player. The service is solid, the coverage national and the prices in the market average. It is a credible alternative to Murfy for those who prioritise the independence of a pure play specialist.

## Practical advice for buying a refurbished cooktop

Six points of vigilance secure the purchase regardless of the chosen site. The logic remains the same as for a more general [refurbished vs used vs new](/en/blog/refurbished-vs-used-vs-new/) arbitrage, with reinforced attention to the specificities of induction cooking.

| Step | Concrete action | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| 1. Check the invoice | Request a nominative invoice mentioning the refurbished status | Activates the legal conformity warranty (24 months) |
| 2. Check the glass | Read the precise description of the cosmetic grade (A, B, C) and inspect photos under raking light | Avoids disappointment at delivery on micro scratches and crack initiations |
| 3. Check built-in dimensions | Confirm the cut-out (typically 560 x 490 mm for a 60 cm) and the depth under the worktop | A mismeasured cooktop requires a worktop redesign |
| 4. Test on receipt | Run each zone individually with a suitable pot, check temperature rise and regulation | 14 day legal withdrawal period for distance sales |
| 5. Keep the packaging | Keep the original packaging for 14 days | Facilitates return if defect detected |
| 6. Check induction compatibility | On an induction cooktop, check the compatibility of cookware (ferromagnetic base) | A non compatible utensil is not a cooktop defect |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying without checking the seller's Trustpilot rating, particularly on marketplace: less than 4 out of 5 or fewer than 100 reviews is a caution signal on an induction cooktop.
2. Confusing refurbished and second hand: second hand from private sellers offers no legal warranty beyond the hidden defect, which is hard to prove and particularly risky on a power electronics board whose defect can reveal itself after several weeks of use.
3. Underestimating the electrical connection: a 60 cm induction cooktop typically requires a dedicated 32 amp line, a 75 cm or 90 cm may require reinforcement of the electrical panel. To be included in the overall installation budget.
4. Not checking touch controls: on a refurbished cooktop, the sensitivity of the touch surface may have drifted. Test each key on receipt and request warranty application in case of erratic response.
5. Not anticipating trade in of the old appliance: free pickup at delivery is a standard service at Murfy and Envie, paid or non existent elsewhere.
6. Neglecting child safety and automatic shut off: these functions must be tested on unpacking, their failure is a legitimate reason to apply the warranty.
