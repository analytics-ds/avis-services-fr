> **At a glance:**
> 1. **Dougs and In Extenso answer two different models.** Dougs targets freelancers and micro-businesses with a lean, transparent accounting offering (200-500 euros per year). In Extenso offers integrated support to growing small businesses with a dedicated advisor (1,500-3,000 euros per year).
> 2. **Dougs excels on cost and simplicity.** Smooth mobile app, streamlined interface, public pricing, setup in 48 hours. Perfect for those who just need to record and file.
> 3. **In Extenso offers advice beyond compliance.** Founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners, 250 offices and 7,500 employees, it covers accounting, tax, payroll, legal, financing and growth support. A true partner if you move to the next growth stage.
> 4. **The winning match depends on your stage.** Freelancer or micro in cruise mode? Dougs. Small business that will hire or face a structural milestone (financing, transfer)? In Extenso. Unsure of your future? In Extenso offers flexibility to move from standard questions to integrated support.

## Dougs: the lean accountant for freelancers

Dougs understood that a growing share of business creators seek fast, transparent, affordable accounting. The model is attractive: sign up, connect your bank data or import documents, Dougs files declarations, you access figures in real time via your phone.

The strength lies in three factors. First, cost: between 200 and 500 euros annually, it is unbeatable. Second, transparency: the price does not change, no surprises, no hidden fees. Third, speed: access in 48 hours and no assigned advisors creating delays.

Dougs shines particularly for two segments. Freelancers with stable activity, few complications (few employees, few fixed assets, simple financing) who just want to record and file without headaches. New entrepreneurs testing an idea who do not want to invest in a heavy firm before knowing if it works.

Dougs' weakness appears as soon as your situation becomes non-standard. You plan to hire an employee, change legal status, seek financing, transfer the activity, restructure compensation to reduce taxes. At that point, the "chat support" model is no longer enough. Dougs can direct you to external partners, but this fragments your relationship.

Another point: Dougs does not anticipate. You must ask the question to get an answer. If you do not know there is a tax or legal opportunity that applies to you, you will miss it. In Extenso, by contrast, scrutinizes your file and proactively suggests opportunities.

## In Extenso: the integrated partner for the growing small business

In Extenso is the opposite structure. Founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners, the firm has grown to 250 offices across France, 7,500 employees. The model: a dedicated regional advisor who manages your accounting, tax, payroll, legal and strategic file.

This model works well because it is integrated. When you face a question about the best way to compensate two partners' contributions, the advisor arbitrates both tax impact, social impact, payroll cost, retirement coverage, dividend taxation, all in coherence. Not three different contacts passing the buck. It seems basic, but it changes everything.

In Extenso excels on growth challenges. If you move from 80k to 500k in revenue, you will need to rethink your structure, payroll, maybe a loan, recruitment. The In Extenso advisor, who has known your situation from the start, can pilot these transitions. Dougs will tell you "that is beyond our scope, figure it out yourself."

[In Extenso also covers business transfer](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-transfer-support/), [financing strategy](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-financing-advice/), [HR and payroll consulting](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/hr-consulting-sme/), legal questions. All through a regional network, meaning physical proximity and understanding of local context.

In Extenso's downside is cost. Starting from 1,500 euros per year for a micro and climbing to 3,000-4,000 for a small business with employees, it is steep when you are still testing or in micro mode. If you are a freelancer and your revenue has plateaued at 30k for three years, you overspend with In Extenso.

## Who should choose what? Three profiles

**You are a freelancer or sole trader, revenue below 50k, stable activity, few questions beyond accounting.** Dougs is your answer. You save 1,200 euros per year and have a solution that matches your need precisely. If your situation becomes more complex (employees, financing, transfer), you switch to In Extenso. It is reversible.

**You are a small business in growth, plan to hire, or think seriously about it.** In Extenso. Your dedicated advisor will anticipate issues and guide you. It will cost more, but it will prevent costly mistakes and missed opportunities. [Business financing for growth](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-financing-advice/) and [strategic growth support](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/growth-strategy-consulting/) will quickly justify the investment.

**You hesitate between your growth prospects and stability.** In Extenso offers flexibility. If you eventually become an SME, your advisor evolves with you. Dougs does not scale the same way.

## The best match depends on your horizon

Do not confuse low price with good value. Dougs is excellent value for a freelancer in cruise mode. It is poor value if you accept its low cost uncritically and miss a 2,000 euro per year tax optimization because no one suggested it.

In Extenso requires more investment, but if you are on a growth trajectory or facing a structural milestone (hiring, financing, transfer), that partnership pays for itself quickly. The [best business consulting firm](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/best-business-consulting-firm/) does not exist in absolute terms. The one matching your development stage does.
