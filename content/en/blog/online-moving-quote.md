## Why go through an online moving quote

Requesting an **online moving quote** has become, since the mid-2010s, the fastest method to compare rates from several moving companies without multiplying phone calls. The principle is simple: a single form sends the moving details (volume, distance, access conditions, date) to a network of partner movers, each of whom responds with a priced quote.

This comparison method complements, without replacing, direct contact with a local mover, as detailed in our article on [how much does moving cost in 2026](/en/blog/how-much-does-moving-cost/), which presents the factors that make the final price vary.

### Information to prepare before requesting a quote

- The estimated volume to be moved, in cubic meters (a 2-room flat averages 20 to 25 m3, a 4-room home 40 to 50 m3)
- The full departure and arrival addresses, with the approximate distance
- Access conditions: floor, presence of an elevator, possibility of parking for the truck
- The desired date and its flexibility (off-peak periods reduce the price by 20 to 30 percent)

## How to read and compare several moving quotes

Comparing moving quotes is not just about looking at the total amount. A complete quote must detail several line items that allow for a genuinely relevant comparison between several proposals.

### Items to systematically check on a quote

1. **The volume used** — check that it corresponds to an actual estimate and not a generic average based on housing type
2. **Included services** — furniture disassembly and reassembly, packing, furniture lift if needed
3. **Insurance** — the mover must have mandatory professional liability insurance, and offer additional "replacement value" insurance for valuable items
4. **Cancellation terms** — a free, no-commitment quote should not generate any fees if it is not signed

> "Moving rates vary by 30 to 50 percent from one provider to another for the same volume and the same distance, which systematically justifies putting providers in competition before signing."
> — Union of Moving Companies, 2025 sector note

## Online comparison tool or local mover: how to decide

National online comparison tools simplify the first step of the search, but their business model relies on a commission paid by the partner mover, ultimately passed on in the price shown to the client. A local mover contacted directly can sometimes offer a lower rate over short distances, particularly for intra-city moves where their local knowledge (parking, access) is an advantage.

The most effective method is to combine both approaches: get 2 to 3 quotes through an online comparison tool, then simultaneously request a quote from a well-rated local company on Google Reviews, in order to have a complete comparison panel before deciding.

## Reducing moving costs through collaborative alternatives

Beyond a classic quote from a moving company, alternative solutions can significantly reduce the bill for tight budgets or small volumes. Our comparison of the [best online storage service](/en/blog/best-online-storage-service-2026/) details temporary storage solutions useful during a move done in several stages, while [peer-to-peer service platforms](/en/blog/best-peer-to-peer-service-platform/) help find occasional help (extra hands, a vehicle) at a lower cost than a traditional company.

### Solutions to reduce a moving bill

- **Self-service van rental**: €200 to €600 total versus €1,200 to €1,500 for a full-service move
- **Moving carpooling platform**: sharing a route with a carrier already on the way, a 40 to 60 percent reduction
- **Peer-to-peer help**: occasional use of extra hands rather than a full professional team
- **Off-peak period**: moving between October and February reduces the rate by 20 to 30 percent compared to the high summer season
