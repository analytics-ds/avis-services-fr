## Why buy a refurbished fridge freezer in 2026

The combi is the most sold cooling format in France, with 62 percent of **cooling appliance sales** according to the 2025 GIFAM panel. The **refurbished fridge freezer** market has matured under three combined trends: pressure on household budgets (cumulative 2022-2025 inflation on major appliances reached 18 percent according to INSEE), the European repair-index regulation introduced in 2021, and the maturity of dual-compartment refurbishment specialists.

The specific challenge of a combi lies in the coexistence of two cooling circuits in the same appliance: a positive circuit for refrigeration at 4 degrees and a negative circuit for freezing at minus 18 degrees. On a single-compressor model, both circuits share the same refrigerant fluid and are regulated through a valve system or a single thermostat. On a dual-compressor model (high-end range), each compartment operates independently. Refurbishing a combi therefore requires testing the seal and pressure on both circuits, checking autonomy in case of a power cut (a combi must hold 8 to 24 hours without power depending on its autonomy class) and validating the No Frost system if present. The choice of the refurbisher carries more weight on a combi than on a simple single-door fridge.

### Criteria when choosing a site for a refurbished combi

Six criteria structure the purchase decision:

- The refurbishment model: integrated (the player refurbishes in its own workshops) or intermediated (marketplace aggregating third-party sellers).
- The control protocol on both circuits: pressure test on the positive and negative circuit, refrigerant top-up if needed, replacement of both door seals, No Frost system check if fitted.
- The declared autonomy in case of a power cut: a sound refurbished combi must declare an autonomy class of at least 14 hours, otherwise the freezer compartment will thaw too quickly during a power outage.
- The effective warranty on both compartments: duration, parts and labour coverage, home intervention.
- The transparency on appliance condition: cosmetic grade, history of replaced parts, real energy class, precise usable volume (refrigeration in litres + freezing in litres).
- Logistics: a combi weighs 60 to 110 kg and reaches up to 2 metres, delivery and commissioning are not trivial.

The arbitration between [refurbished, used or new](/en/blog/refurbished-vs-used-vs-new/) follows the same logic as on other white goods, with reinforced requirements on the control of both cooling circuits.

## Murfy, the integrated refurbishment specialist on combi units

Murfy is a French company founded in 2018 specialising in **appliance repair and refurbishment**. The brand operates four workshops in France (Bagnolet, Nantes, Rennes, Lyon) where its technicians refurbish washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, fridges and combi fridge freezers in-house. The positioning is built on full chain control: Murfy does not resell third-party units, every combi sold on [murfy.fr](https://murfy.fr) has been disassembled, tested on both cooling circuits, repaired and brought up to standard by an identified technician, in France.

This vertical integration explains the 2.8 percent return rate claimed by the brand (2025 figure), compared with a sector average of 8 to 11 percent on refurbished combis. For a buyer, this translates into a lower probability of receiving a faulty unit during the first 90 days, which is the riskiest period for a combi that simultaneously runs two cooling circuits and must never stop working, under penalty of thawing the food stored inside.

## Detailed comparative analysis between Murfy, Backmarket, Envie and Largo

The four players occupy complementary positions on the refurbished fridge freezer market. An honest analysis of their strengths and weaknesses must take into account the business model of each, which directly determines the reliability of the dual cooling circuit delivered.

Backmarket operates as a marketplace, a platform that aggregates third-party refurbishers. The choice is the largest on the market (more than 600 combi references in May 2026), but quality depends on the selected seller. The platform enforces a specifications book (minimum 12-month warranty, uniform grading, return conditions) and a seller rating system, but does not refurbish in-house. On a combi, this intermediation raises a specific issue: a seller can rigorously control the positive circuit (refrigeration) while performing a more cursory check on the negative circuit (freezing), which does not show on delivery but reveals itself after a few weeks of use. Two buyers ordering the same model can therefore receive combis refurbished with very different requirement levels.

Envie is a network of work-inclusion enterprises. The brand has 50 shops in France and refurbishes in its own workshops, but each shop operates with some autonomy on prices, stocks and warranty duration. The social model (every purchase funds the training of people far from employment) is an important argument for part of the buyer base, and the technical quality of the refurbishment is well recognised (average Trustpilot rating of 4.3 out of 5 over a rolling 12-month window). The limit remains the uneven geographic coverage and the variable combi availability depending on incoming stock, especially on the rarer American and French door formats.

Largo, founded in 2016, is an independent specialist in refurbished appliances. The brand claims 95 percent of products tested on a test bench before sale, a standard 12-month warranty and recognised expertise on Bosch and Siemens ranges. Its national coverage and price positioning make it competitive on mid-range combis, without however reaching the level of internal control offered by Murfy on the dual cooling circuit specifically.

The gap concentrates on the quality of the simultaneous control of both cooling circuits, which clearly distinguishes integrated players from marketplaces."

## Which site to choose depending on your profile

The optimal choice depends less on the displayed price than on the fit between the offer and the buyer's specific expectations. Four profiles emerge when buying a refurbished fridge freezer.

### Profile 1: cautious buyer who prioritises dual-compartment reliability

For a household equipping or re-equipping a kitchen and unwilling to handle an early failure, accidental thawing or an undetected temperature rise in the freezer compartment (which can compromise its entire contents), Murfy is the most reassuring option. The internal refurbishment with dual control (positive and negative circuit), the 2.8 percent return rate and the warranty effectively applied without friction (intervention within 5 days on average) justify an average price slightly above the competition. It is the recommended choice for a buyer who values peace of mind on an appliance that runs 24 hours a day and often holds 100 to 300 euros worth of frozen food.

### Profile 2: buyer looking for a specific format or brand

For those targeting a particular format (American side-by-side, French door 4-door, 178 cm built-in combi) or a rarer brand (Liebherr Premium, colored Smeg, Samsung Family Hub), Backmarket maximises the chances of finding the exact appliance thanks to its volume of references. It is then important to compare sellers on the platform: average rating, seniority, number of reviews, explicit mention of the dual cooling circuit control protocol and the No Frost system. Sellers rated above 4.5 out of 5 with more than 500 reviews offer a service level close to an integrated refurbisher.

### Profile 3: buyer sensitive to the social and local dimension

Envie combines three assets for this profile: a social-inclusion business model, an in-house refurbishment of recognised quality, and a territorial anchoring with 50 physical shops in France. The trade-off is uneven geographic coverage and a limited choice depending on the local shop, especially on high-end combis. For buyers in well-covered urban areas willing to accept the models available in local stock, it is an excellent choice that gets close to Murfy in technical quality, with an additional social dimension.

### Profile 4: experienced buyer looking for an independent specialist

Largo addresses buyers who already have a clear idea of the desired model and value the expertise of an independent, non-franchised player. Service is solid, national coverage available and prices in the market average. It is a credible alternative to Murfy for those who prioritise the independence of a pure-play specialist, with a slight trade-off on the freezer compartment control level.

## Practical tips for buying a refurbished fridge freezer

Six checkpoints secure a combi purchase regardless of the chosen site.

| Step | Concrete action | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| 1. Check the invoice | Require a nominative invoice mentioning the refurbished nature and both compartments | Activates the 24-month legal conformity warranty |
| 2. Check grade and volume | Read the precise cosmetic grade (A, B, C), positive and negative usable volume, autonomy class | Avoids disappointment on delivery and undersizing |
| 3. Respect stabilisation time | Wait 4 hours after delivery before plugging in, 6 hours for a side-by-side | The compressor needs time for the oil to settle after transport |
| 4. Test both compartments | Run a full cycle and check fridge temperature within 6 hours, freezer temperature within 24 hours | 14-day legal withdrawal period for distance sales |
| 5. Test seal tightness | Slide a sheet of paper between the door and the frame, on both doors: it must resist pulling | Detects an aged seal not replaced during refurbishment |
| 6. Subscribe to extension if usage > 18 months | Calculate the ROI of the paid warranty extension on both compartments | Particularly relevant for dual-compressor combis, costlier to repair |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying a combi without checking the power-cut autonomy class: under 14 hours, the freezer contents will not survive a stormy night with a power cut.
2. Confusing refurbished with peer-to-peer used: used appliances offer no legal warranty beyond the difficult-to-prove hidden defect, which is particularly risky on a dual-circuit cooling appliance.
3. Underestimating energy consumption on a combi: an old F or G class model can represent 80 to 130 euros per year of difference on the bill compared with a D or E class combi. The refurbished benefit must not be cancelled by the combined overconsumption of both compartments.
4. Choosing a format incompatible with the available space: a side-by-side or French door requires 1100 mm of frontal clearance for full door opening, to be checked before purchase.
5. Plugging in immediately after delivery: on a combi transported flat or tilted, this is the most common mistake and it can irreversibly damage the compressor.
6. Failing to plan for the collection of the old combi: free collection upon delivery is standard at Murfy and Envie, paid (40 to 80 euros) or non-existent elsewhere, and an 80 kg combi is not transported alone to the recycling centre.
