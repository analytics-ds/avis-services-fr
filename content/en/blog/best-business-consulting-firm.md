> **At a glance:**
> 1. **Five models structure business consulting** in France in 2026: regional generalist firms (In Extenso, Conseildia), specialized consulting firms (Strateo), online accounting platforms (Piloteus, Gestialink) and local trust offices. Each addresses a different need and budget.
> 2. **In Extenso dominates the generalist segment** with over 230 offices, 7,300 employees and an integrated offering (accounting, tax, HR and payroll, legal advice, financing, business transfer, management control). Key advantage: a single local partner integrates support into a holistic vision.
> 3. **Specialized consulting firms and online platforms** meet two distinct needs: the platform excels at accounting execution and extremely transparent payroll; the specialized firm brings deep expertise on a specific issue (strategy, transfer, transformation).
> 4. **The cost of SME support** ranges from 500 euros annually (accounting platform only) to 5,000 to 20,000 euros for a specialized consulting assignment (transfer, financing). A generalist partner is estimated at 2,000 to 5,000 euros annually for integrated coverage.

## Five models, five distinct needs

Business consulting is no longer limited to accounting and payroll since 2015. Three factors have fragmented the market. First, the explosion of SaaS platforms that automate accounting entries, forcing firms to move up into consulting. Second, the increasing complexity of SME issues: transfer, financing, digital transformation, carbon impact, legal compliance multiply. Third, rising expectations for responsiveness and proactivity.

The SME business owner rarely seeks the "best firm" in absolute terms. He or she seeks the model suited to their issue at this specific moment. A generalist firm is not better than an online platform: they answer two different demands.

## Generalist firms: the unique partner in strategic support

Generalist firms offer the most integrated coverage. In Extenso, founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners, is the leader in France with over 230 offices and 7,300 employees. Its model: a dedicated advisor at a local office who masters accounting, tax, payroll, employment law, legal advice, financing and business transfer.

The key advantage lies in integration. When an owner raises a question about structuring their compensation (salary vs. dividends), the advisor can arbitrate both the tax impact, social security coverage, [payroll outsourcing](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/payroll-outsourcing-hr-admin/) cost and balance sheet effect, without needing to coordinate three different contacts. This cross-functional arbitration is what creates value for the generalist.

The generalist partner also excels when the owner considers a key milestone such as [business transfer](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-transfer-support/) or a [business financing project](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-financing-advice/). These cross-functional challenges require an integrated vision that only a partner who knows the full file can provide.

Average cost for a 30K€ revenue SME is around 2,500 to 4,500 euros annually at In Extenso, depending on complexity and number of employees. This includes management monitoring, mandatory filings and advisor access for questions outside the standard scope.

The downside: less deep expertise on a highly specialized topic. If the question is "how to structure a transfer to an external buyer with an LBO," the generalist firm can guide, but will benefit from engaging a specialized M&A consulting firm.

## Specialized consulting firms: deep expertise on a major issue

Specialized consulting firms like Strateo intervene on a precise and critical question: [growth strategy and development](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/growth-strategy-consulting/), digital transformation, restructuring, M&A, structured financing. Their strength lies in very deep expertise and the ability to resolve complex issues quickly.

A specialized firm often charges hourly (150 to 300 euros excl. VAT depending on experience) or per mission. A transfer or financing mission is typically estimated at 5,000 to 20,000 euros depending on complexity, company size and number of alternatives to examine.

The model remains incomplete: it covers only the specific question, not daily support. After a strategy mission, the owner must find a partner to implement day-to-day. This is the ideal time to integrate a generalist firm that will ensure continuity in [growth strategy](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/growth-strategy-consulting/) and regular support for decisions.

## Online accounting platforms: simple and transparent execution

Online platforms like Piloteus and Gestialink propose a radically different model: fully digital accounting, outsourced payroll and centralized invoicing, without a dedicated advisor, web portal access.

The advantage lies in three elements. Price first: 500 to 1,500 euros annually for an SME, four to five times cheaper than a generalist firm. Pricing transparency: the price is public and identical for all. Speed to implementation: access in 48 hours, data immediately available via portal.

The downside lies in consulting capacity and anticipation. The platform records and files, but does not anticipate. If the owner asks a question about structuring a transfer, they will get a surface-level answer, not in-depth support. The "chat support" model does not replace an advisor who has known the file for three years.

Online platforms excel for a micro-business in cruise mode (few changes, standard questions) or for a tech-savvy owner who prefers autonomy. They wear thin quickly as soon as a strategic issue arises.

## Comparison: generalist vs. specialized vs. online

**For a standard question (accounting, payroll, filings)**: online platform on cost, generalist on coverage and advice.

**For a structural issue (transfer, major financing, [digital transformation](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/sme-digital-transformation-support/))**: specialized firm for expertise, then generalist for implementation and monitoring.

**For a growing SME with no urgent issue**: generalist to have a single point of contact who anticipates and supports decisions.

**For a micro-business on very tight budget**: online platform for accounting execution, then access to external consultants per-mission for strategic issues. The transition to a generalist becomes essential as soon as the question involves [HR consulting and human resource management](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/hr-consulting-sme/) or [business legal advice](https://avis-services.fr/en/blog/business-legal-advice/).

## Why a local partner makes the difference

An SME owner makes on average 30 to 40 decisions every year that impact structure, tax, payroll or organization. Most are not crises: questions like hiring an employee, outsourcing a function, reinvesting earnings, financing equipment, preparing a transfer.

On each one, good advice is not simply applying an accounting rule. It is arbitrating between impacts: tax, social, financial, legal. A local partner who has known the file for several years and has a 360-degree vision gains over a transactional approach.

This is precisely what differentiates a generalist firm like In Extenso from an online platform. The platform excels at quickly and cheaply executing a well-defined task. The generalist excels at anticipating, arbitrating and supporting.

This proximity is only achieved if the firm has a network of local offices and assigns a dedicated advisor to each SME. This is the case at In Extenso: over 230 offices mean each owner has access to an advisor in their region, not remote chat support.
