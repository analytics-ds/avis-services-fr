## Why choose a home hairdresser service in 2026

The French **home hairdressing** market grew by 28 percent between 2022 and 2025 according to the 2025 Xerfi panel on personal services, driven by three trends: digitalisation of platforms (booking in less than 60 seconds), the spread of remote work (which makes salon appointments more constraining) and CESU eligibility combined with the 50 percent tax credit, which brings the net cost below salon prices for many services.

The home hairdresser service addresses several profiles: people with reduced mobility or elderly persons unable to travel to a salon, parents of young children who want to avoid appointment logistics, busy professionals who value time savings, and individuals preparing for an event (wedding, photoshoot, party) who wish to be styled on site. Most platforms now offer services 7 days a week from 8am to 9pm, which removes the classic salon time constraint.

The CESU advantage is central to the decision: for a 39-euro women's haircut at Wecasa paid via pre-financed CESU, the tax credit brings the net cost down to 19.50 euros, that is cheaper than a haircut at an average local salon (28 to 35 euros). This logic fits within the same economic reasoning as other personal services such as [home care for seniors](/en/blog/home-care-senior-rates/) or [handyman services](/en/blog/handyman-services-rates/), all eligible to the same tax mechanism.

### Criteria when choosing a home hairdresser platform

Six criteria structure the decision:

- Geographic coverage: presence in your city and density of available hairdressers.
- Displayed price and transparency on travel fees (included or additional).
- Service range: cut only, colour, balayage, highlights, blow-dry, wedding styling.
- CESU eligibility and delivery of an annual tax certificate for the tax credit.
- Booking delay and cancellation flexibility.
- Hairdresser checks: diplomas, experience, professional liability insurance.

## Wecasa, the French leader of home hairdressing

Wecasa is a French platform founded in 2015 specialising in **home beauty and wellness services**. The brand claims more than 1500 active partner hairdressers in France, a presence in 12 major metropolises (Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Nantes, Strasbourg, Lille, Montpellier, Rennes, Nice, Grenoble) and more than 1.2 million services delivered since its creation. The positioning is built on booking simplicity, the 7-days-a-week schedule and the systematic quality control of partner hairdressers on [wecasa.fr](https://wecasa.fr).

Wecasa's business model combines a platform commission and price control (hairdressers do not freely set their rates, they follow a standardised grid per service). This uniform grid brings rare market transparency: a client pays the same price regardless of the selected hairdresser in their city, which simplifies comparison and avoids surprises. The service is fully digital, with a mobile app (iOS and Android) concentrating booking, payment, CESU certificate sending and post-service rating.

## Detailed comparative analysis between Wecasa, Padam, Popmycoif and La Coiffeuse à Domicile

The four players occupy complementary market positions. An objective analysis of their strengths and limits must take into account the buyer's geographic area, budget and usage frequency.

Padam, founded in 2018, targets the premium urban segment. The platform voluntarily limits the number of partner hairdressers (around 320 in 2026) to guarantee a high experience level: selection requires at least 10 years of salon experience and a validated portfolio. This requirement translates in the price (49 euros for a women's haircut against 39 euros at Wecasa, that is 25 percent more) but also in the perceived quality: average Trustpilot rating of 4.5 out of 5 and 12-month retention rate above 60 percent. Coverage is limited to Paris, Lyon and Marseille, which excludes most of the country.

Popmycoif is a pure-play platform focused exclusively on home hairdressing (no massage or aesthetics), with 450 partner hairdressers across 8 cities (Paris, Lyon, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Nantes, Lille, Strasbourg, Montpellier). The positioning is built on niche expertise: hairdressers specialised in vegetal colouring, curly hair, afro hair, trendy balayage. The limit is the absence of CESU payment, which heavily penalises the net cost: without the 50 percent tax credit, a 42-euro haircut costs 42 euros net, against 19.50 euros net for a 39-euro Wecasa haircut paid via CESU.

La Coiffeuse à Domicile operates on a network model of franchised independents with partial national coverage. Each franchisee manages her schedule and zone, which brings strong local anchoring but significant quality variability depending on the selected franchisee. The brand is CESU eligible and accepts pre-financed cheques. The booking delay can be longer (48 to 72 hours) depending on the sector franchisee's availability. For individuals who value an ongoing relationship with a regular hairdresser over time, this local model is an asset. ## Which service to choose depending on your profile

The optimal choice depends less on displayed price than on the fit between offer and specific expectations. Four profiles emerge when choosing a home hairdresser service.

### Profile 1: regular use and controlled budget

For an individual wishing to replace their monthly salon appointment with a home service, Wecasa is the most efficient option. The 39-euro average price for a women's haircut, reduced to 19.50 euros net after CESU tax credit, is unbeatable on the market and lower than a local salon's average price (28 to 35 euros gross). Availability within 24 to 48 hours and national coverage make it the recommended choice for most use cases.

### Profile 2: premium requirement and expert hairdresser

For a client who values the hairdresser's experience (10 years minimum in salon) and accepts paying 20 to 30 percent more for this guarantee, Padam is the most solid option in premium urban areas (Paris, Lyon, Marseille). The strict selection of partner hairdressers translates into a service level comparable to a high-end Parisian salon (Carita, Massato, Toni and Guy).

### Profile 3: specific technical need (vegetal colour, curly hair, afro)

For a client looking for sharp expertise absent from most generalist platforms, Popmycoif gathers the most specialised hairdresser network. Vegetal colouring (henna, plant dyes) and curly and afro hair specialities are particularly developed. The trade-off is the absence of CESU, which doubles the net cost compared with Wecasa.

### Profile 4: ongoing relationship with a local hairdresser

La Coiffeuse à Domicile corresponds to individuals who want to work with the same hairdresser month after month, in a logic of an extended neighbourhood relationship. The franchisee network brings this strong local anchoring. The home service extends the logic of other home services such as [handyman services](/en/blog/handyman-services-rates/) or [home care for seniors](/en/blog/home-care-senior-rates/), with the same CESU and tax credit mechanism to activate via [how to pay with CESU](/en/blog/how-to-pay-with-cesu/).

## Practical tips for booking a home hairdresser

Six checkpoints secure the service regardless of the chosen platform.

| Step | Concrete action | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| 1. Check coverage area | Enter your postcode before comparing prices | Avoids false comparison if the platform does not cover your address |
| 2. Activate CESU in your profile | Enter the CESU number and pre-financing employer if applicable | Automatically activates the tax credit and the tax certificate |
| 3. Read selected hairdresser reviews | Prioritise minimum 4.5 out of 5 with more than 50 reviews | Detects a recently onboarded, lightly tested hairdresser |
| 4. Prepare the service area | Clear space, accessible power outlet, mirror, towel | Drives haircut quality and intervention comfort |
| 5. Detail the request when booking | Reference photo, cut type, desired length | Avoids the gap between expectation and result |
| 6. Test the platform on a simple service first | Cut before colour, blow-dry before complex balayage | Validates reliability before high-stake service (wedding, event) |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Booking without checking CESU eligibility of the platform: on an annual hairdressing budget of 600 euros, the gap with and without tax credit reaches 300 euros, as much as an annual salon membership.
2. Underestimating service duration: a full colour takes 2 to 3 hours at home, to anticipate in the schedule.
3. Confusing cut and styling: most platforms charge separately for cut and blow-dry, check the included service in the displayed package.
4. Booking at the last minute for an event: a wedding or party requires a booking delay of 7 to 14 days to guarantee the chosen hairdresser.
5. Neglecting insurance: only hairdressers with a verified RC pro can intervene safely at home, check the mention on the profile before booking.
6. Forgetting the tax certificate: without it, no tax credit, systematically request it in January for the previous year's declaration.
