## What is a concierge service in Paris?

A **concierge service in Paris** refers to a service that handles, on behalf of an individual, a homeowners' association, or a rental property owner, everyday logistical and organizational tasks: parcel reception, bookings, short-term rental property management, or high-end personal assistance. Paris has the highest density of concierge services in France, driven by three factors: the size of the secondary residence and pied-à-terre stock, the boom in short-term furnished rentals since 2015, and an international clientele already accustomed to this type of service in other capitals (London, New York, Dubai).

The term covers very different realities depending on the context, which explains why it is hard to identify "the" best service: a hotel concierge, a building concierge, and a private concierge answer radically different needs and budgets.

### The three main families of Parisian concierge services

- **Luxury concierge for individuals**: bespoke personal assistance (bookings, travel, VIP access), billed through a premium subscription
- **Rental management concierge**: full handling of a short-term rental property (Airbnb, Booking), billed on commission
- **Neighborhood or building concierge**: shared practical services (parcels, dry cleaning, small errands), billed through an accessible monthly subscription

## Luxury concierge: for which profile?

Luxury concierge services target a clientele that delegates the organization of their daily life in exchange for a premium subscription or access included with certain high-end bank cards (Visa Infinite, World Elite Mastercard, American Express Centurion). The service covers reservations at fully-booked, starred restaurants, obtaining tickets for sold-out events, organizing bespoke travel, and 24/7 assistance in case of an emergency.

### What a luxury concierge service includes

- Priority reservations at fully booked restaurants and venues
- Complete travel arrangements (flights, hotels, private transfers)
- Access to private events (previews, private sales, evenings)
- One-off administrative and logistical assistance

The 2025 report from the Federation of Concierge Companies estimates the French premium concierge market at several tens of millions of euros, with double-digit annual growth driven by the rise of an international clientele residing in or visiting Paris.

## Rental management concierge: the most structured segment

This is the most mature segment of the Parisian market, born in the wake of Airbnb and Booking starting in 2015. It answers a precise need: freeing a landlord from the operational management of their short-term rental property (listing, calendar, guest greeting, cleaning, maintenance).

### How a rental management concierge gets paid

| Model | How it works | Advantage | Limitation |
|--------|-----------------|----------|--------|
| Commission on revenue | 15 to 25% of gross rental income | Aligns provider's interest with performance | Costly at high booking volumes |
| Fixed monthly fee | €250 to €600 per month | Predictable cost | No incentive to optimize pricing |

For a full breakdown of pricing grids and the main players, our dedicated article on [Airbnb concierge pricing](/en/blog/airbnb-concierge-pricing/) details the calculation methodology and profitability by booking volume.

## Neighborhood and building concierge: the accessible segment

Less well known but growing fast, this segment offers shared practical services at the scale of a building or neighborhood: parcel reception when the resident is away, dry cleaning, small errands, and connections with trusted tradespeople. The business model relies on an accessible monthly subscription, often between 50 and 120 euros, sometimes partly or fully covered by the homeowners' association.

> "Corporate and neighborhood concierge services form the most dynamic segment of the French personal services market, with estimated growth of over 10 percent per year since 2020."
> — Federation of Concierge Companies, 2025 sector report

This model resembles, in some respects, the [personal care services](/en/blog/best-personal-care-company/) offered by major national brands, with the difference that neighborhood concierge remains focused on one-off logistical tasks rather than recurring human assistance.

## How to choose your concierge service in Paris

The choice should be based on the actual need rather than the provider's reputation. A concierge service built for short-term rentals will bring no value to an individual simply looking for occasional help with parcels, and vice versa.

### 3-step method to compare offers

1. **Precisely define the need** — rental management, neighborhood service, or high-end personal assistance
2. **Check geographic coverage** — some providers only operate in a few districts
3. **Request at least 3 detailed quotes** — comparing the billing model (commission, flat fee, subscription) and commitment terms (3 to 12 months depending on the provider)

For one-off home services that often accompany a concierge service (housekeeping, [handyman services](/en/blog/handyman-services-rates/)), it is possible to compare specialized providers alongside a general concierge to optimize the overall budget.

<details>
<summary>How much does a concierge service cost in Paris?</summary>

A concierge service in Paris ranges from 50 to 150 euros per month for a neighborhood concierge subscription (parcels, dry cleaning, small errands), 15 to 25 percent of rental revenue for short-term rental management, and 300 to over 2,000 euros per month for a dedicated high-end private concierge with 24/7 availability. One-off tasks (an errand, a booking) are generally billed between 20 and 50 euros each.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the difference between a building concierge and a luxury concierge?</summary>

A building concierge handles parcel reception, visitor greeting and common area upkeep for all residents of a building, on a shared model billed to the homeowners' association. A luxury concierge is an individual, bespoke service (restaurant reservations, travel arrangements, access to private events) billed directly to the individual, often through a premium subscription or a high-end bank card that provides access to it.

</details>
