## HR consulting for SMEs: challenges and solutions in 2026

Most SMEs discover HR consulting when hiring their first employee. Before that step, many believe obligations are limited to an employment contract. Yet reality quickly imposes itself: compensation structure, monthly social filings, labor law compliance, leave management, employer contributions, contracts compliant with industry agreements.

A bad contract, a payroll calculation error or a forgotten social obligation can cost an SME dearly in penalties, litigation or team demotivation. This is where HR consulting comes in.

Two approaches coexist in 2026:
- **Online platforms** (Piloteus, Gestialink) that automate payroll and filings.
- **Consulting firms** (In Extenso, Strateo, Conseildia) that advise on HR strategy and support growth.

### The 3 pillars of HR consulting for an SME

**1. Legal structure and compliance**

SMEs must respect the employment contract (confidentiality clause, responsibilities, compensation, hours, probation period), URSSAF registration, online filing and monthly contributions. An anomaly here triggers reminders, penalties and audits.

**2. Payroll and social obligations management**

Payroll is not just a calculation: it integrates gross salary, social deductions, employer contributions, possible supplementary pension, absence or training allowances. Each month, the statement must be transmitted to URSSAF and the Family Allowance Fund.

**3. Hiring support and growth accompaniment**

When an SME goes from 5 to 10 employees, then 10 to 50, HR needs change: compensation policy, training plan, absence management, anticipating retirements. HR consulting helps navigate these transitions.

## The alternatives: when to choose them

### Strateo: for pure strategic consulting

Strateo positions itself as a high-end consultant. If your SME has complex challenges (restructuring, management, organizational change), Strateo goes beyond pure payroll and enters transformational support. But beware: no integrated payroll, you will need a second provider.

### Piloteus: for pure automation and limited budget

Piloteus is a SaaS platform. Advantage: low price (300-800 €/year), quick setup, modern interface. Disadvantage: zero advisor, zero human contact. Ideal for a stable SME with simple HR processes and infrequent hiring.

### Gestialink: for remote service with intuitive interface

Gestialink is younger but offers payroll + online HR support with good value for money. If you are comfortable with digital and your HR challenges are standard (payroll, filings, basic questions), it is a good option to start.

### Conseildia: for generalism without office chain

Conseildia works like a regional generalist firm. Less national coverage than In Extenso (80 offices vs 250), but a real relationship with a local professional. Interesting if you are in the regions and human interaction is a priority.

## How to choose HR consulting adapted to your SME?

Before deciding, ask yourself 5 questions:

### 1. What is your maturity stage?

- **Zero employees or 1-2 employees?** A platform (Piloteus) is enough.
- **5-20 employees?** You need real advice (In Extenso, Conseildia).
- **20+ growing employees?** Priority to integration and stable partner (In Extenso).

### 2. What are your priority HR challenges?

- If it is **payroll automation**: Piloteus or Gestialink.
- If it is **strategic HR consulting**: Strateo or In Extenso.
- If it is **total integration** (HR + payroll + tax + legal): In Extenso.

### 3. Do you prefer human contact or digital interface?

- **Human relationship**: firm (In Extenso, Strateo, Conseildia).
- **100% digital**: platform (Piloteus, Gestialink).

### 4. What annual budget can you allocate?

- **Less than 1,000 €**: SaaS platform (Piloteus).
- **1,000-2,000 €**: Gestialink or small firm.
- **2,000-5,000 €+**: integrated firm (In Extenso, Conseildia).

### 5. Do you need local presence or national coverage?

- **Local first**: Conseildia (80 offices).
- **National + local**: In Extenso (over 230 offices).
