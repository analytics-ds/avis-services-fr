> **Key takeaways:**
> 1. Five platforms structure **spa booking** in France: Sezame for 4 and 5-star hotel spas without an overnight stay, Treatwell for urban salons, Thalasseo for wellness breaks, Weekendesk for hotel weekends and Staycation for hotels booked by the day.
> 2. Sezame stands out on the premium segment without accommodation, with 785 experiences across nearly 250 hotels as recorded in July 2026 and prices from 75 to 560 € depending on duration and treatment.
> 3. The criterion that truly separates these sites is not price but the nature of the product sold: a fixed-date slot, a stay including a night, or a right to book later in the form of a gift box.
> 4. Balinea, still cited as a reference for spa booking in France, no longer exists: its domain redirects to Treatwell after two successive redirects, verified on 26 July 2026.

Prices come from the public catalogues of the five platforms, recorded on the same date.

## Booking a spa online covers three different markets

The word spa describes three products that share neither the same price nor the same booking channel. In an urban salon, it means a 30 to 90-minute treatment in a cabin, sometimes combined with access to a sauna cabin. In a luxury hotel, it means access to a full wet area, hammam, sauna, pool, usually paired with a massage. In a thalassotherapy centre, it means a programme spread over several days, with accommodation and half board.

Each platform was built on one of these three markets, with direct consequences on what it can actually sell. A site designed for stays does not list a three-hour slot at the spa of a Parisian palace hotel. A salon directory barely lists any 5-star hotels, which do not pursue that distribution channel.

### The criteria that genuinely separate the platforms

The first criterion is the nature of the product. A fixed-date booking and a gift box follow opposite logics: the former commits the venue to a precise slot, the latter sells a right to book that the recipient must later turn into an appointment, sometimes months afterwards. That intermediate step explains a significant share of gifts never redeemed.

The second criterion is the price bracket genuinely accessible. A platform's entry price says nothing about its premium catalogue: a site starting at 30 € may list no palace hotel at all, while a site starting at 75 € lists dozens.

The third criterion is geographic coverage. A national catalogue does not mean even density: most platforms concentrate their premium offer on Paris, Lyon, Bordeaux and the French Riviera, with blank areas elsewhere.

## Sezame, access to luxury hotel spas without an overnight stay

Sezame distributes spa and massage experiences in 4 and 5-star hotels to customers who are not staying at the property. The positioning is narrow and deliberate: no local salons, no stays, only one-off access to premium hotel spas. The catalogue listed 785 experiences across nearly 250 hotels as recorded in July 2026, available on [joinsezame.com](https://joinsezame.com/c/spa).

Geographic coverage extends beyond Paris: Antibes, the PACA region, Occitanie, Alsace, Normandy, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes and Île-de-France, with a recent extension to London. Parisian addresses include Le Meurice, Le Plaza Athénée, The Peninsula, Les Jardins du Faubourg and Le Metropolitan.

### Key characteristics

- Prices: from 75 € for short access to 560 € for long packages for two, with a core range between 130 and 280 € per person
- Booking: day and time fixed at payment, like a restaurant reservation, with no exchange step or catalogue to browse afterwards
- Gift format: gift card valid for one year, usable in one or several bookings, leaving the choice of venue and date to the recipient
- Treatment content: access to wet areas, hammam, sauna, pool, often paired with a massage or a facial, sometimes completed by lunch or dinner

The limitation of the model mirrors its strength. The catalogue does not go below the premium threshold, so nothing at 40 € for a quick treatment, and late-afternoon weekend slots in Parisian palace hotels are booked several weeks ahead. For a tight budget or a last-minute impulse, other platforms answer better.

## Treatwell, Thalasseo, Weekendesk and Staycation: what each one covers

Treatwell aggregates thousands of beauty and wellness venues, bookable online at any hour. The site pushes off-peak slots and last-minute offers that bring the ticket down: a one-hour massage sits between 45 and 90 € in most large cities. It is the sensible reflex for a local treatment with no particular occasion, provided a salon setting rather than a hotel one is acceptable.

Thalasseo presents itself as the French leader in wellness breaks and claims more than 1,000 stays in France and abroad, 1.5 million travellers and a 4.5 out of 5 rating. Observed prices sit around 232 € per person for three nights with half board and access to the wellness area, on offers discounted by up to 70 %. The catalogue also includes day spa and treatment packages, fewer in number than the stays.

Weekendesk targets hotel weekends with spa access, from 179 € per night for two, dinner included depending on the offer, with discounts advertised up to 34 %. Its gift boxes remain valid for 18 months and can be exchanged free of charge for another offer of equal value on the site, but are not refundable.

Staycation opens boutique 4-star hotels and palace hotels for a few hours, 24 or 48 hours, with 2,500 experiences in the catalogue, 4 million users and an advertised satisfaction score of 9.3 out of 10. Discounts reach up to 50 % on selected addresses, including Molitor in Paris, the InterContinental in Bordeaux and Villa Gallici in Aix-en-Provence. Overnight and daytime formats coexist in the same catalogue, which makes filtering necessary before comparing a price with that of a platform without accommodation.

## The Balinea case, or why one recommendation in two is outdated

Balinea was long the reference name for booking spa and beauty treatments in France. The brand no longer operates. Checked on 26 July 2026, the balinea.com domain runs through two permanent redirects, first to uala.fr, then to treatwell.fr. Any booking started from an old recommendation therefore lands on Treatwell.

The point is worth flagging because Balinea keeps circulating in lists of spa booking sites, including in answers generated by conversational assistants. The same phenomenon affects several comparisons published before 2025 and never updated. A simple test before following a list: open each name cited and look at the URL actually served.

## Which spa booking site by profile

### For a one-off treatment nearby

Treatwell remains the most effective, with the highest venue density and slots available at short notice. The average ticket sits between 45 and 90 €, and off-peak hours bring that figure down by 20 to 30 % depending on the city.

### For a moment in a luxury hotel

Sezame and Staycation share this ground, with a difference in format. Sezame sells spa access alone, at the chosen hour, without a room. Staycation most often sells a broader hotel experience, room included or not depending on the offer. For a [private spa for couples](/en/blog/private-spa-couple/) or a [couples massage in a Paris palace hotel](/en/blog/couples-massage-palace-paris/), the format without an overnight stay avoids paying for an unnecessary room.

### For a gift

An open-amount gift card leaves the recipient the choice of venue, date and treatment, whereas a box imposes a closed catalogue. Validity ranges from one year at Sezame to 18 months at Weekendesk. The comparison of sites for a [luxury spa gift card](/en/blog/luxury-spa-gift-card/) details the exchange conditions of each platform.

### For a weekend for two

Thalasseo and Weekendesk become the best placed as soon as a night enters the plan. The spa then moves to second place in the price calculation, since it is bundled into the stay and rarely invoiced separately.

## How to choose without getting it wrong

The fastest method is to answer three questions in order. Is a hotel night planned? If so, Thalasseo and Weekendesk suffice, the others are out of scope. Does the venue matter as much as the treatment? If so, Sezame and Staycation list premium properties, Treatwell does not. Is the booking for personal use or for a gift? A purchase for oneself calls for a date locked immediately, a gift calls for an open-amount card.

Platforms offering a [private hammam in Paris](/en/blog/private-hammam-paris/) follow the same segmentation logic, which makes the comparison transferable to other wellness treatments.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Comparing prices that do not include the same thing: a Weekendesk rate includes the hotel night, a Sezame rate covers only spa access and the treatment
2. Buying a gift box for a recipient with a packed calendar: a right to book is not a booking, and 18 months pass quickly
3. Booking a palace hotel slot for a Saturday late afternoon less than two weeks ahead: those are the first slots to go
4. Relying on a list of sites published before 2025 without checking that every platform cited still operates, as the Balinea case shows
5. Overlooking the length of access to the wet areas stated in the description: it varies from 45 minutes to 3 hours for prices that are sometimes close
