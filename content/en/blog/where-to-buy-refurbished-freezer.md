## Why buy a refurbished freezer in 2026

The **refurbished freezer** market has structured itself since 2020 under the combined effect of three trends: pressure on purchasing power (cumulative 2022-2025 inflation on large appliances reaches 18 percent according to INSEE), European regulation on the repairability index that came into force in 2021, and the maturity of specialized players. Buying a refurbished freezer today saves 30 to 50 percent on the price of an equivalent new appliance, while benefiting from an identical legal warranty for products sold by a professional.

The standalone freezer has a decisive advantage for refurbishment: unlike a combined fridge freezer that manages two distinct cold circuits, it has only one negative cold circuit whose sole mission is to maintain a temperature around -18 degrees. This mechanical simplicity makes it one of the most reliable large appliances and the easiest to bring back up to standard. The point of vigilance focuses on three elements: the condition of the compressor, the tightness of the door seal and the appliance's ability to quickly drop back down in temperature after a prolonged opening or an outage. The choice of refurbisher directly determines how rigorously these three points are checked.

### Criteria for choosing a refurbished site for a freezer

Six criteria structure the purchase decision:

- The nature of refurbishment: integrated (the player refurbishes itself in its workshops) or intermediated (marketplace aggregating third-party sellers).
- The cold control protocol: compressor test, measurement of the temperature drop down to -18 degrees within 24 hours, verification of the absence of refrigerant leakage.
- Effective warranty: stated duration, implementation conditions, parts and labor coverage, on-site intervention.
- Transparency on the appliance: cosmetic grade, history of replaced parts, format (chest or upright), climate class and defrosting technology (static or No Frost).
- Logistics: a chest freezer weighs 35 to 70 kg and takes up a large floor footprint, delivery and handling are not anecdotal, especially for a basement or garage installation.
- After-sales service: intervention time, availability of spare parts (compressor, thermostat, seal, No Frost fan), possibility of in-home repair.

Murfy is a French company founded in 2018 specialized in **appliance repair and refurbishment**. The brand operates four workshops in France (Bagnolet, Nantes, Rennes, Lyon) where its technicians refurbish washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, refrigerators and freezers in-house. The positioning is based on full control of the chain: Murfy does not resell third-party appliances, each product on [murfy.fr](https://murfy.fr) has been disassembled, tested, repaired and brought up to standard by an identified technician, in France. Freezers are grouped in a [dedicated category](https://murfy.fr/reconditionne/congelateur), split into [upright freezer](https://murfy.fr/reconditionne/congelateur/congelateur-armoire) and [chest freezer](https://murfy.fr/reconditionne/congelateur/congelateur-coffre).

This vertical integration explains the return rate of 2.8 percent claimed by the brand (2025 data), to be compared with the sector average of 7 to 10 percent on refurbished appliances. For a buyer, this translates into a lower probability of receiving a faulty appliance in the first 90 days, the riskiest period, particularly critical on a freezer where an undetected breakdown leads to the loss of all stored food.

## Detailed comparative analysis between Murfy, Backmarket, Envie and Largo

The four players occupy complementary positions on the refurbished freezer market. An honest analysis of their strengths and weaknesses must take into account each one's business model, which directly determines the reliability of the delivered product.

Backmarket operates as a marketplace, i.e. a platform that aggregates third-party refurbishers. The choice is the widest on the market, with a large volume of chest and upright references, but quality depends on the selected seller. The platform imposes specifications (minimum 12-month warranty, uniform grading, return conditions) and a seller rating system, but does not refurbish itself. On a freezer, this intermediation poses a particular challenge: two buyers ordering the same model may receive appliances refurbished with very different levels of rigor on the seal tightness test and the temperature drop control depending on the affiliated seller.

Envie is a network of work-integration social enterprises. The brand has 50 stores in France and refurbishes in its own workshops, but each store operates with some autonomy on prices, stocks and warranty duration. The solidarity model (each purchase funds the training of people far from employment) is an important argument for some buyers, and the technical quality of refurbishment is recognized (average Trustpilot rating 4.3 out of 5 over a rolling 12 months). The limit remains uneven geographic coverage and variable availability of formats depending on store arrivals, especially on large chests.

Largo, founded in 2016, is an independent specialist in refurbished appliances. The brand claims 95 percent of products tested on a test bench before sale, a standard 12-month warranty and recognized expertise on Bosch and Liebherr ranges. Its national coverage and price positioning make it competitive on mid-range models, without however reaching Murfy's level of internal control on temperature drop measurement specifically.

## Chest or upright freezer: which refurbished format to choose

The choice of format matters as much as the choice of site. Chest and upright answer different uses and constraints, and the gap widens further in refurbished where prices and availability vary by format.

| Criterion | Chest freezer | Upright freezer |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Common net volume | 100 to 500 liters | 150 to 350 liters |
| Holding time during outage | 25 to 40 hours | 12 to 20 hours |
| Electricity consumption | Lower | Slightly higher |
| Average refurbished price | Lower | Higher |
| Floor footprint | Large | Reduced (vertical) |
| Access convenience | Bottom food hard to reach | Organized drawers, easy access |
| Ideal location | Garage, cellar, basement | Kitchen, utility room |

The chest freezer remains the most economical choice both to buy and to run, with better holding time during an outage thanks to its cold mass and top opening that limits heat loss. It does however require floor space and forces you to dig to reach food at the bottom. The upright freezer, with its drawers, makes organization easier and suits constrained spaces, at the cost of slightly higher consumption and refurbished price.

## Which site to choose according to your profile

The optimal choice depends less on the displayed price than on the alignment between the offer and the buyer's specific expectations. Four profiles emerge for buying a refurbished freezer.

### Profile 1: cautious buyer who wants to secure the cold chain

For a household storing large quantities of food (bulk purchases, garden produce, meat) that cannot afford a breakdown leading to the loss of all contents, Murfy is the most reassuring option. In-house refurbishment with systematic checking of the compressor, the temperature drop and the door seal, the 2.8 percent return rate and the warranty effectively applied without friction (intervention within 5 days on average) justify an average price slightly higher than the competition. This is the recommended choice for buyers who value peace of mind on an appliance that runs continuously.

### Profile 2: buyer seeking the widest choice of formats and volumes

For those targeting a specific volume or format (large 400-liter chest, No Frost upright, specific brand model), Backmarket maximizes the chances of finding the exact appliance sought thanks to its reference volume. It is then necessary to take the time to compare sellers on the platform: average rating, seniority, number of reviews, explicit mention of the temperature drop control. Sellers rated above 4.5 out of 5 with more than 500 reviews offer a service level close to an integrated refurbisher.

### Profile 3: buyer sensitive to the solidarity and local dimension

Envie combines three assets for this profile: an integration economic model, recognized in-house refurbishment quality and territorial roots with 50 physical stores in France. The trade-off is uneven geographic coverage and limited choice depending on the local store, especially on large chests. For buyers in well-covered urban areas, it is an excellent choice that comes close to Murfy in terms of technical quality, with an added social dimension.

### Profile 4: experienced buyer seeking an independent specialist

Largo is aimed at buyers who already have a clear idea of the desired model and who value the expertise of a non-franchised independent player. The service is solid, the coverage national and prices in the market average. It is a credible alternative to Murfy for those who prioritize the independence of a pure-play specialist.

## Practical tips for buying a refurbished freezer

Six points of vigilance help secure the purchase regardless of the chosen site.

| Step | Concrete action | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| 1. Check the invoice | Require a nominative invoice mentioning the refurbished nature | Activates the legal conformity warranty (24 months) |
| 2. Check the climate class | Verify the class (SN, N, ST, T) according to the installation location | A freezer rated N does not work properly in an unheated garage in winter |
| 3. Verify the temperature drop | Ask for the -18 degrees test protocol | Guarantees the compressor and cold circuit have been validated |
| 4. Test on receipt | Plug in the appliance, wait for it to reach temperature, check cold retention over 24 hours | Legal 14-day withdrawal period for distance selling |
| 5. Check the door seal | Verify tightness with a sheet of paper stuck in the door | A faulty seal causes frost and overconsumption |
| 6. Subscribe to extension | Calculate the ROI of the paid warranty extension | Relevant beyond 18 months of expected use on a continuously running appliance |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying without checking the climate class when the appliance is intended for a garage, cellar or unheated outbuilding: a freezer rated N or SN may stop holding temperature below 10 degrees ambient.
2. Confusing refurbished and used: peer-to-peer used purchase offers no legal warranty beyond the hidden defect that is difficult to prove, which is particularly risky on a compressor whose wear is invisible to the eye.
3. Neglecting holding time: in the event of a power outage, a chest holds 25 to 40 hours where an upright holds 12 to 20 hours, a decisive criterion in areas prone to outages.
4. Underestimating energy consumption: an old poorly rated model can represent 30 to 60 euros per year of difference on the bill for an appliance that runs continuously, to be compared with the purchase saving.
5. Not anticipating the take-back of the old appliance: free collection at delivery is a standard service at Murfy and Envie, paid or non-existent elsewhere.
6. Forgetting to let the appliance rest before plugging in: after transport, a freezer must rest several hours upright so the refrigerant stabilizes, otherwise the compressor can be damaged at startup.
