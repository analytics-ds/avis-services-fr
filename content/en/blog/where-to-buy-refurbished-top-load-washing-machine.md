## Why choose a refurbished top load washing machine in 2026

The **refurbished top load washing machine** meets a specific demand: a narrower footprint (40 to 45 cm wide versus 60 cm for a front loader), top opening convenient for tight spaces and a contained budget. ## Practical tips for choosing a refurbished top load washer

Choosing a **refurbished top load washing machine** follows a five step logic structured around the objective criteria of the French market.

1. Define the required capacity: 5 kg for 1 to 2 people, 6 kg for 3 people, 7 to 8 kg for 4 people and more
2. Compare the cosmetic grade: grade A (like new), grade B (micro scratches), grade C (visible scratches, lowest price)
3. Check parts and labor warranty and whether home interventions are possible
4. Calculate the total cost (purchase plus delivery plus installation), not only the displayed entry price
5. Check spare parts availability for the chosen brand (Bosch, Whirlpool, Indesit, Vedette remain the most available)

As reminded in our comparison [best place to buy a refurbished front load washing machine](/en/blog/best-place-buy-refurbished-front-load-washing-machine/), the warranty criterion is decisive for an appliance whose expected lifespan is 12 to 15 years.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying based on the displayed entry price without calculating delivery and installation costs: the final gap can exceed 60 euros
2. Overlooking the home intervention warranty: a workshop return is a hidden cost in case of breakdown
3. Choosing grade C to save 30 euros on a top loader placed in a very visible spot, where durability is identical but appearance matters
