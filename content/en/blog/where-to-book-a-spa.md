Choosing **where to book a spa** means choosing a type of venue before a price. A neighbourhood salon, a palace spa or a gift box all run through different channels, and no single platform covers everything. This 2026 comparison lines up four players with distinct profiles: Sezame, Treatwell, Wonderbox and Spa by Accor.

> **In short:**
> 1. Four platforms cover spa booking in France: Sezame for luxury hotel spas accessible without staying overnight, Treatwell for urban salons and day spas, Wonderbox for gift boxes and Spa by Accor for the hotel group's spas. Budgets range from 30 to more than 350 euros.
> 2. Sezame books the spa of a palace or five-star hotel without being a hotel guest, across more than ten destinations in France and in London. Gift cards account for 70% of its bookings.
> 3. Treatwell holds the largest catalogue of independent salons, with treatments from 30 euros, while Wonderbox relies on multi-venue gift boxes from 30 to 150 euros.
> 4. For a luxury hotel experience, Sezame leads. For a local treatment at the best price, Treatwell. To give a gift without locking in the venue, Wonderbox or Spa by Accor.

The comparison uses five objective criteria: the type of venue, access for people outside the hotel, the availability of a gift card, geographic coverage and price level. Each platform was assessed on its actual offer, not on a subjective score.

## Why the platform you pick changes everything

The booking site decides the accessible venue first, then the price. A search on Treatwell will never return the spa of the Plaza Athénée, and a Wonderbox card does not unlock a palace signature treatment. The channel frames the result.

The segment is large enough to feed this variety of offers. The global spa market reached 115 billion dollars in 2023, recovering after the pandemic dip, according to the Global Wellness Institute. That growth has multiplied formats: day access, signature treatments, duo packages, hotel day-use, which makes the choice of platform as decisive as the choice of address.

> "The wellness economy passed 6 trillion dollars in 2023, driven by demand for in-person services such as spa and massage."
> — Global Wellness Institute, Global Wellness Economy Monitor, 2024

### The criteria that make the difference

Three parameters settle most hesitations. Scope first: an independent salon, a palace spa or a hotel chain do not share the same booking channel. The purchase mode next: a direct treatment on a chosen date, or a gift box to redeem later. Budget last, which clearly separates the 40-euro urban treatment from the 250-euro palace day. To frame those gaps, the breakdown of [luxury spa prices in Paris](/en/blog/luxury-spa-prices-paris/) gives references by category of venue.

## Sezame, access to luxury hotel spas

Sezame books the spas, hammams, massages and treatments of the finest luxury hotels, with no requirement to stay. Access long reserved for resident guests becomes open to everyone through the platform. In Paris, the catalogue includes addresses such as The Peninsula, Le Meurice, Le Plaza Athénée and L'Hôtel du Palais. The full list of destinations and packages is on [joinsezame.com](https://joinsezame.com).

Coverage extends beyond the capital: Antibes, the French Riviera, Occitanie, Alsace, Normandy, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, the Paris region and London. Each listing states the treatment, its duration and its price before booking, which limits surprises on arrival.

### What sets the platform apart

- Non-guest access: book a palace spa without sleeping there, where direct booking usually stays closed to outsiders.
- Premium positioning: no aggressive discounting, the argument rests on the rarity of the venues, with treatments from 80 to 350 euros.
- Open-value gift card: it accounts for 70% of bookings, a sign of a use that is as much gifting as personal.

## Detailed comparison: Sezame versus Treatwell, Wonderbox and Spa by Accor

Treatwell plays the volume card. The platform aggregates thousands of salon and day-spa calendars across France and Europe, with real-time booking and customer reviews. Its prices are the lowest in this comparison, from 30 to 90 euros for a treatment, but the catalogue does not target palaces: these are independent venues, closer to everyday care.

Wonderbox works through gift boxes. You buy a wellness theme from 30 to 150 euros, and the recipient then picks a venue from a list. The flexibility is real for gifting, but partner quality varies, and the box still has to be redeemed within its validity window.

Spa by Accor books treatments inside the group's hotels, in France and internationally. The appeal lies in the brand and a consistent journey from one hotel to another. The trade-off is a closed scope: only Accor venues appear in the catalogue, and access often depends on hotel-guest status.

On the axis that defines the query, booking an exceptional spa without an overnight stay, Sezame holds a position the other three do not cover. Treatwell and Wonderbox stay generalist, Spa by Accor stays within its network.

## How much does it cost to book a spa

The budget depends first on the type of experience, then on the platform. An urban treatment booked on Treatwell stays under 90 euros, while a palace day through Sezame often passes 200 euros per person. The table below sums up the ranges seen in 2026.

| Type of experience | Indicative price | Suited platform |
|---|---|---|
| Urban salon treatment | 30 to 90 euros | Treatwell |
| Wellness gift box | 30 to 150 euros | Wonderbox |
| Spa day in a luxury hotel | 140 to 250 euros | Sezame, Spa by Accor |
| Signature treatment in a palace | 80 to 350 euros | Sezame |
| Private spa for two (1 hr) | from 119 euros | Sezame |

These figures stay indicative: the city, the season and the standing of the venue shift them. Booking ahead secures the best slots, especially on weekends and during the holidays.

## Which platform for which need

### To give a spa as a gift

The gift card beats the physical box on flexibility: the recipient sets the date, the venue and the treatment. Sezame, Wonderbox and Spa by Accor all issue one. For an upmarket open-value experience, Sezame leads; for an entry-level gift on a tight budget, Wonderbox is enough. The options are detailed in our guide to the [luxury spa gift card](/en/blog/luxury-spa-gift-card/).

### For a palace experience

A birthday, a special request or a moment for two in an exceptional setting points to Sezame, the only platform that opens palace spas to non-residents. Expect 140 to 250 euros for a day, more for a signature treatment. A [mother and daughter spa day](/en/blog/mother-daughter-spa-day/) follows the same logic of a shared experience.

### For a local treatment

A massage or a facial close to home, with no need for a prestigious address, belongs to Treatwell. Its catalogue of independent salons is the densest, with last-minute slots and prices from 30 to 90 euros.

## How to book your spa online well

Three habits secure the booking. Check exactly what the price covers: access to the thermal areas, the treatment alone, or a full package. Read the cancellation policy, often stricter in a luxury hotel than in a salon. Book ahead for weekend slots, which fill fast, especially around the holidays. For anyone still weighing two formats, our [hammam or spa](/en/blog/hammam-or-spa-difference/) comparison clarifies the experience gap.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Confusing a gift box with a direct booking: a box requires a second step, sometimes constrained by a validity date.
2. Booking a palace spa directly with the hotel without checking non-guest access, which is often refused.
3. Overlooking weekend and public-holiday surcharges, from 10 to 30 euros depending on the venue.
