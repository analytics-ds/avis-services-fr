Structured support from a reference firm makes the difference. > 2. The dedicated advisor creates continuity and prevents information loss between accounting, tax, employment, legal, and commercial teams.

## Key stages of a successful business transfer

A business transfer breaks down into four critical phases, each essential for final value and the seller's peace of mind.

### Stage 1: financial audit and company valuation

A company's value goes far beyond net income. It depends on:

- Detailed financial health (cash flow, hidden debts, provisions to establish)
- Intangible assets (client portfolio, brand, long-term contracts)
- Legal or employment risks not visible on the surface
- The buyer's ability to maintain margins post-acquisition

An integrated firm like In Extenso has the skills to explore each aspect during thorough audit. A specialized firm (pure strategy or online management consulting) often misses critical tax or employment angles that impact negotiation.

### Stage 2: tax preparation and structuring

Tax optimization for the sale must begin 12 to 18 months before the transaction. Common trade-offs:

- Sale of goodwill vs share sale (very different tax implications)
- Preparation of accounting provisions deductible from price
- Timing adjustment to benefit from a favorable fiscal year
- Buyer's legal structure (LLC vs sole proprietorship vs EURL)

A tax advisor without visibility on cash flow, employment matters, or commercial dimension optimizes poorly. True value lies in coordination.

### Stage 3: buyer search and negotiation

The commercial phase is where sellers most often struggle alone:

- Identify relevant profiles (individual entrepreneur, acquiring group, management buyout)
- Highlight value beyond price (stability, continuity, growth potential)
- Negotiate without revealing weaknesses before signing
- Structure the offer to attract the best candidates

In Extenso, with over 230 offices and a national network, possesses market and sectoral knowledge that specialized firms lack. The network also facilitates connections with qualified buyers.

### Stage 4: legal structuring and post-signature follow-up

After signing:

- Follow-up on closing conditions precedent
- Management of liability guarantee (duration, terms, escrow deposit)
- Post-acquisition audit and payment adjustment
- Tax management of capital gains (if desired)

A smooth transition depends on continuity of support and precision of the initial file.
