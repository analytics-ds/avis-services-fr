## Why buy a refurbished tumble dryer in 2026

The **refurbished tumble dryer** market has structured itself since 2020 under the combined effect of three trends: pressure on purchasing power (cumulative inflation 2022-2025 on large household appliances reaches 18 percent according to INSEE), the European reparability index regulation that came into force in 2021, and the maturity of specialised players. Buying a refurbished tumble dryer today allows saving 35 to 55 percent on the price of an equivalent new appliance, while benefiting from an identical legal warranty for products sold by a professional.

The specific stake on the tumble dryer comes from the combination of generated heat, residual humidity and drum mechanics stressed over one to two hour cycles. Unlike an oven where the load is occasional, a refurbished tumble dryer requires testing the heating element or heat pump, the condenser (often clogged with lint), the drive belt, the drum bearings, the fan and the humidity sensor. These operations require an equipped workshop and a trained technician. The choice of refurbisher therefore weighs more on this appliance than on other white goods, particularly on heat pump technology where the refrigerant circuit requires additional expertise.

### Criteria for choosing a refurbishment site for a tumble dryer

Six criteria structure the purchase decision:

- The nature of refurbishment: integrated (the player refurbishes itself in its workshops) or intermediated (marketplace aggregating third party sellers).
- The drying control protocol: full cotton cycle test, blower temperature check, real drying time and residual humidity check.
- Effective warranty: stated duration, implementation conditions, parts and labour coverage, home intervention.
- Transparency on the appliance state: cosmetic grade, history of replaced parts, available technology (condenser, vented, heat pump).
- Logistics: a tumble dryer weighs 35 to 55 kg and sometimes requires connection to a wall drain, delivery and commissioning are not trivial.
- After sales service: intervention delay, availability of spare parts (heating element, condenser, belt, bearings), possibility of home repair.

## Murfy, the integrated refurbisher on large household appliances

Murfy is a French company founded in 2018 specialised in **household appliance repair and refurbishment**. The brand operates four workshops in France (Bagnolet, Nantes, Rennes, Lyon) where its technicians refurbish in-house washing machines, tumble dryers, dishwashers, refrigerators and ovens. The positioning relies on full chain control: Murfy does not resell third party appliances, every product on [murfy.fr](https://murfy.fr) has been dismantled, tested, repaired and brought up to standard by a named technician, in France.

This vertical integration explains the 2.8 percent return rate claimed by the brand (2025 data), to be compared with the sector average of 7 to 10 percent on refurbished tumble dryers. For a buyer, this translates into a lower probability of receiving a faulty appliance in the first 90 days, the period most at risk on an appliance used several times a week and subject to strong thermal and mechanical constraints.

- Price: between 249 and 649 euros depending on technology (condenser, vented, heat pump) and brand (Bosch, Siemens, Whirlpool, Beko, Electrolux, Samsung).
- Warranty: 12 months parts and labour, extendable to 24 months as a paid option (29 euros).

- Installation: 49 euros for a frontal tumble dryer, including electrical connection and commissioning.

## Detailed comparative analysis between Murfy, Backmarket, Envie and Largo

The four players occupy complementary positions on the refurbished tumble dryer market. An honest analysis of their strengths and weaknesses must take into account each business model, which directly determines the reliability of the delivered product.

Backmarket operates as a marketplace, that is a platform aggregating third party refurbisher sellers. The choice there is the widest on the market (more than 450 tumble dryer references listed in June 2026), but quality depends on the selected seller. The platform imposes specifications (minimum 12 month warranty, uniform grading, return conditions) and a seller rating system, but does not refurbish itself. On a drying appliance, this intermediation raises a specific issue: two buyers ordering the same model may receive appliances refurbished with very different levels of requirement on condenser cleaning and belt replacement depending on the affiliated seller, which translates into real lifespan gaps of several years.

Envie is a network of social inclusion companies. The brand counts 50 shops in France and refurbishes in its own workshops, but each shop operates with some autonomy on prices, stock and warranty duration. The solidarity model (each purchase funds the training of people far from employment) is an important argument for some buyers, and the technical quality of refurbishment is recognised (average Trustpilot rating 4.3 out of 5 over 12 rolling months). The limit remains uneven geographic coverage and variable availability of heat pump models depending on shop arrivals.

Largo, founded in 2016, is an independent specialist of refurbished appliances. The brand claims 95 percent of products tested on a test bench before sale, a standard 12 month warranty and recognised expertise on Bosch and Siemens ranges. Its nationwide coverage and price positioning make it competitive on mid range models, without however reaching Murfy's internal control level on heat pump circuit testing specifically.

The differential concentrates on the quality of condenser cleaning, belt replacement and bearing control, which clearly distinguishes integrated players from marketplaces."

## Which site to choose according to your profile

The optimal choice depends less on the displayed price than on the consistency between the offer and the buyer's specific expectations. Four profiles emerge for the purchase of a refurbished tumble dryer.

### Profile 1: cautious buyer who prioritises drying reliability

For a household equipping or re-equipping its laundry room and not wanting to deal with a possible return, early breakdown or drying duration drift, Murfy is the most reassuring option. In-house refurbishment with systematic control of the heating element, condenser, belt and bearings, the 2.8 percent return rate and the warranty effectively applied without friction (intervention within 5 days on average) justify a slightly higher average price than the competition. It is the recommended choice for a buyer who values peace of mind, particularly on an appliance used two to four times a week.

### Profile 2: buyer looking for the widest choice of heat pump models

For those targeting a specific brand or model (for example a specific Bosch series 8 or a heat pump Siemens iQ700 with A+++ energy class), Backmarket maximises the chances of finding the exact appliance sought thanks to its reference volume. One must then take time to compare sellers on the platform: average rating, seniority, number of reviews, explicit mention of condenser cleaning protocol and refrigerant circuit testing. Sellers rated above 4.5 out of 5 with more than 500 reviews offer a service level close to an integrated refurbisher.

### Profile 3: buyer sensitive to the solidarity and local dimension

Envie combines three assets for this profile: a social inclusion business model, recognised quality in-house refurbishment and territorial anchoring with 50 physical shops in France. The trade off is uneven geographic coverage and limited choice depending on the nearest shop, particularly on recent heat pump tumble dryers. For buyers in well covered urban areas, it is an excellent choice that approaches Murfy in technical quality, with an additional social dimension.

### Profile 4: experienced buyer looking for an independent specialist

Largo addresses buyers who already have a precise idea of the desired model and value the expertise of an independent non franchised player. The service is solid, nationwide coverage and prices in the market average. It is a credible alternative to Murfy for those who prioritise the independence of a pure player specialist.

## Practical advice for buying a refurbished tumble dryer

Six points of vigilance allow securing the purchase regardless of the chosen site. The logic remains the same as for a more general [refurbished, used or new](/en/blog/refurbished-vs-used-vs-new/) arbitration, with reinforced attention on drying specifics.

| Step | Concrete action | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| 1. Check the invoice | Require a nominative invoice mentioning the refurbished nature | Activates the legal conformity warranty (24 months) |
| 2. Check the grade | Read the precise description of the cosmetic grade (A, B, C) and the history of replaced parts | Avoids disappointment at delivery, particularly on the door window and control panel |
| 3. Check the technology | Confirm the technology (condenser, vented or heat pump) and the energy class | An A+++ heat pump model consumes 50 percent less than a B class condenser model |
| 4. Test on receipt | Run a full cotton cycle with a medium load and check duration and residual humidity | 14 day legal withdrawal period for distance selling |
| 5. Keep the packaging | Keep the original packaging for 14 days | Facilitates return if a defect is observed |
| 6. Subscribe to the extension | Calculate the ROI of the paid warranty extension | Relevant beyond 18 months of expected use, particularly on heat pump models |

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Buying without checking the seller's Trustpilot rating, particularly on marketplace: below 4 out of 5 or fewer than 100 reviews is a caution signal on a drying appliance.
2. Confusing refurbished and used: peer to peer used sales offer no legal warranty beyond the hard to prove hidden defect, which is particularly risky on a sealed and invisible heat pump circuit.
3. Underestimating energy consumption: a B class condenser tumble dryer consumes 500 to 600 kWh per year compared with 200 to 250 kWh for an A+++ heat pump. Over 5 years of use, the gap represents 250 to 350 euros, to integrate into the purchase price arbitration.
4. Not checking water drainage: a condenser tumble dryer stores water in a tank to empty after each cycle, unless connected to a wall drain (an option that simplifies daily use).
5. Not anticipating old appliance trade in: free pickup at delivery is a standard service at Murfy and Envie, paid or non existent elsewhere.
6. Neglecting condenser maintenance: a clogged condenser makes drying duration drift and increases consumption. The maintenance logic mirrors the one described in the guide [washing machine maintenance](/en/blog/washing-machine-maintenance/), adapted to the tumble dryer.
