## Why financing reshapes an SME's trajectory

Many entrepreneurs wait too long before structuring their financing strategy. Financial forecasting is more than a banking formality. It is the first internal management tool. Understanding how much free cash will remain after three years of growth changes strategic action plans.

### Loan file structuring, a critical step

An unstructured financing request is rejected in three days by a bank. A well-organized file, following clear specifications with comparative data and justified assumptions at each step, passes the credit committee. The difference lies in three key documents:

- **Financial forecasting**: income statement, balance sheet, financing schedule for 3 or 5 years, with realistic and documented assumptions.
- **Presentation file**: executive summary, company history, management team, commercial strategy, competitive positioning.
- **Supporting documents**: client contracts, growth proof, market studies, letters of intent from investors or partners.

Each of these three elements carries weight in the banker's decision. Leaving gaps allows doubt to creep in. An online platform can generate the skeleton of a forecast, but it cannot validate commercial strategy or anticipate real risk zones.

### Access to funding sources

SMEs often ignore the five existing funding sources:

1. **Traditional bank loan**: the slowest source (30 to 45 days) but least costly in terms of equity dilution.
2. **Public grants and subsidies**: France Relance, Bpifrance, regional authorities, local governments. Highly varied and frequently overlooked.
3. **Territorial subsidies**: regions, departments, agglomerations depending on sector and location.
4. **Business angels and investors**: direct fundraising if strong growth potential.
5. **Crowdfunding and participatory finance**: lengthy process (4 to 6 months) but ideal for commercial validation.

Only a generalist firm truly masters this complete map. Financing specialists know it well. Online platforms list it without understanding current access in each region.

### Loan file structuring at In Extenso

The typical process includes:

1. **Initial diagnosis**: cash position audit, past profitability analysis, growth factor identification.
2. **Forecast building**: commercial assumptions validated with the owner, scenarios tested (pessimistic, realistic, optimistic).
3. **File preparation**: executive summary, team presentation, strategic justification.
4. **Funding source selection**: mix of bank loans, public grants, equity contributions based on needs and repayment capacity.
5. **Banker support**: file presentation, question responses, condition negotiation.
6. **Post-financing monitoring**: tracking dashboards, budget alerts, adjustments as needed.

A dedicated advisor at In Extenso steers the entire process, ensuring coherence between loan file structure and the SME's accounting.

## Strateo: strategy consulting only

Strateo is a strategy and business development consulting firm. Its scope covers commercial strategy, market segmentation, positioning and growth plans.

But Strateo does not address financing. No financial forecasting, no banker relationships. An SME working with Strateo to define growth plans must engage another partner to validate financial viability with a bank.

Cost: 3,000 to 8,000 euros depending on scope. Duration: 4 to 8 weeks.

## Piloteus: online forecasting platform

Piloteus is a SaaS financial forecasting solution. The company enters growth assumptions (revenue, margins, investments) and the tool generates income statement, balance sheet and financing schedules. Very useful for initial assessment.

Limitations: the tool does not validate assumption reality, does not prepare a complete file, does not support banker relationships.

Cost: 150 to 300 euros per month depending on plan.

## Gestialink: generalist SME platform

Gestialink is a web platform supporting SMEs in administrative management, HR and tax compliance. It includes a financing section listing applicable public grants by sector and region.

Limitations: informational listing only, no application support, no file structuring.

Cost: 100 to 200 euros per month depending on modules.

## Conseildia: regional generalist accounting firm

Conseildia is a small accounting firm operating in four regions. It offers accounting, tax, HR and general consulting, including financing.

Advantage: local advisor, slightly lower price than In Extenso, solid regional expertise.

Limitation: loss of network effect. No access to specialist pools, no connections with sector partners. For 500,000 euro fundraising, having a 20-person firm versus 7,500 creates a real gap.

Cost: 1,200 to 2,000 euros for loan structuring.
